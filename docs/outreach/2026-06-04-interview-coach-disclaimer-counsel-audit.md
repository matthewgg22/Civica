## Audit findings (8, ordered by severity)

### F1 — `cdss.ca.gov` is the state agency, not "your county CalFresh office" (HIGH)

The current phrasing — *"contact your county CalFresh office (cdss.ca.gov)"* — conflates two distinct entities. CDSS is the California **state** Department of Social Services (oversees the program); CalFresh is **county-administered** by 58 separate county welfare departments. Telling the applicant to "contact your county office" and pointing them at the state URL sends them to the wrong front door, and a CA legal partner will flag this on first read.

**Verified:** [`cdss.ca.gov/county-offices`](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/county-offices) is the canonical CDSS-hosted directory of all 58 county offices, including CalFresh-specific phone numbers where the county lists one (e.g., Sonoma's `(877) 699-6868`). That's the correct pointer.

**Suggested rewrite (EN):** *"contact your local CalFresh office (find yours at cdss.ca.gov/county-offices)"*
**Suggested rewrite (ES):** *"comunícate con tu oficina local de CalFresh (encuéntrala en cdss.ca.gov/county-offices)"*

### F2 — "Binding answers" is unusual phrasing for an applicant audience (MEDIUM)

*"For binding answers about your CalFresh application"* — "binding" carries contract-law connotations that don't quite fit a benefits context. The applicant isn't seeking a binding contract; they're seeking an authoritative determination or case-specific guidance.

**Suggested rewrite (EN):** *"For answers about your specific case"* OR *"For an official determination on your application"*
**Suggested rewrite (ES):** *"Para respuestas sobre tu caso específico"* OR *"Para una determinación oficial sobre tu solicitud"*

### F3 — AI is the source of feedback; the disclaimer doesn't say so (MEDIUM-HIGH)

The current copy says *"Practice prompts and AI feedback are illustrative."* That mentions AI, but doesn't disclose that the practice prompts AND feedback are AI-generated end-to-end. Given the rising legal exposure around AI in benefits work (CFPB Circular 2023-03; FTC AI guidance; multiple state AG positions), counsel will likely want the disclaimer to name this risk explicitly so applicants understand what they're consuming.

**Suggested rewrite (EN, replaces last sentence):** *"Practice questions and feedback are generated by AI — they are not reviewed by a person, may contain errors, and may not reflect your actual eligibility, benefit amount, or county-specific procedures. Don't use them as your basis for filing or responding to your county."*
**Suggested rewrite (ES):** *"Las preguntas de práctica y los comentarios son generados por IA — no son revisados por una persona, pueden contener errores, y pueden no reflejar tu elegibilidad real, monto del beneficio, o los procedimientos específicos de tu condado. No los uses como base para presentar tu solicitud o responder a tu condado."*

This is the longest single addition; counsel may want it shorter, but it's the highest-protection version to start the conversation from.

### F4 — "May not match your situation" is too soft (LOW-MEDIUM, folded into F3)

*"They may not match your situation"* is vague — what part might not match? The suggested F3 rewrite spells it out (eligibility, benefit amount, county procedures), which is what a counsel partner will likely want anyway.

### F5 — Call out that the legal-aid path is FREE (LOW, applicant-impact)

*"Reach a California legal aid organization (lawhelpca.org)"* — many applicants don't know LawHelpCA is no-cost. Adding "free" lowers the barrier to the most-protective referral.

**Suggested rewrite (EN):** *"reach a free California legal aid organization (lawhelpca.org)"*
**Suggested rewrite (ES):** *"contacta una organización de asistencia legal gratuita en California (lawhelpca.org)"*

### F6 — Verify `lawhelpca.org` URL is canonical (LOW, operator-side check)

Agent fetches against `lawhelpca.org` and `www.lawhelpca.org` failed today (TLS + connection-reset). LawHelpCA is a real, federally-funded LSC referral portal — the URL is almost certainly correct, but the operator should confirm in a browser before counsel sees the doc. If LawHelpCA's canonical hostname differs (e.g. `lawhelpcalifornia.org`), update the disclaimer.

### F7 — 2-1-1 California: confirm phone vs URL primacy (LOW)

The disclaimer says *"call 2-1-1 California"* — that's correct as a phone instruction, and [`211ca.org`](https://211ca.org) confirmed itself as the canonical statewide referral service. The phone format `2-1-1` is the universal short code; counsel may want to also include the alternate dial `1-800-339-9226` for users on systems that don't route 211. Optional — ask counsel.

### F8 — Spanish parity is generally good (LOW)

The ES version mirrors the EN closely. Two small notes for the partner's reviewer:

- *"asistencia legal de California"* — both *"de California"* and *"en California"* work; ES copy in other Civica surfaces uses *"en California"* (geographic), but either is acceptable.
- *"pueden no aplicar a tu situación"* maps to *"may not apply to your situation"* (slightly different sense than EN *"may not match"*); if F3's rewrite is adopted, the EN/ES diverge again and need a fresh translation pass.

> This is a practice tool, not legal advice. For answers about your specific CalFresh (SNAP) case, contact your local CalFresh office (find yours at cdss.ca.gov/county-offices), call 2-1-1 California, or reach a free California legal aid organization (lawhelpca.org). Practice questions and feedback are generated by AI — they are not reviewed by a person, may contain errors, and may not reflect your actual eligibility, benefit amount, or county-specific procedures. Don't use them as your basis for filing or responding to your county.

**ES:**

> Esto es una herramienta de práctica, no asesoría legal. Para respuestas sobre tu caso específico de CalFresh (SNAP), comunícate con tu oficina local de CalFresh (encuéntrala en cdss.ca.gov/county-offices), llama al 2-1-1 California, o contacta una organización de asistencia legal gratuita en California (lawhelpca.org). Las preguntas de práctica y los comentarios son generados por IA — no son revisados por una persona, pueden contener errores, y pueden no reflejar tu elegibilidad real, monto del beneficio, o los procedimientos específicos de tu condado. Word count: EN current ~75 → suggested ~110. Counsel may want to shorten; the long form is offered as the high-protection starting point.

The **compact** variant (*"Practice tool — not legal advice."*) is adequate and probably doesn't need counsel attention.

## Out-of-disclaimer questions to flag to counsel

These aren't disclaimer wording — but a CA legal partner reviewing this surface will probably ask about them, and pre-staging the answers shortens the round-trip.

1. **TCPA-consent disclosure.** Does Civica's Interview Coach send SMS or place outbound calls? If yes (or planned), the disclaimer should reference the TCPA consent flow. If no, confirm "no — voice/SMS isn't part of this surface" up front.
2. **Civil-rights / non-discrimination notice.** USDA / FNS rules require beneficiaries-facing materials carry the standard non-discrimination statement. The Interview Coach is a *practice tool* not a benefits-application surface, but counsel may want the boilerplate at the surface boundary anyway.
3. **Per-county data accuracy.** The Interview Coach is single-state (CA); the practice questions are state-level. Some county procedures vary (e.g., LA County has its own InfoLine; SF has HSA-specific intake). Should the disclaimer name "county-specific procedures may differ" explicitly? F3's rewrite does this; confirm with counsel.
4. **Audio/voice processing.** If the coach records audio answers and sends them off-device for processing, a separate audio-consent disclosure is needed. Confirm scope.
5. **Data retention.** Where do practice answers + AI feedback persist? Device-local only (a la the SSN posture from #423) or server-side? Counsel will want to know.
6. **AI substitution warning.** Is there any path in the product where the AI feedback could be mistaken by the user for an actual county determination? If yes, an in-flow modal disclaimer (not just the footer) is worth a counsel discussion.
7. **"Not legal advice" sufficient?** The current copy declares "not legal advice." Some legal-aid orgs prefer the stronger formulation "this is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship." Counsel call.
