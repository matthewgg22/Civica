# CalFresh for CCC + CSU students

## The rule changed

As of June 2026, half-time degree-seeking students at California Community Colleges, CSUs, and UCs qualify for CalFresh automatically — no work-hours rule, no work-study requirement, no EOPS gate. Many students who were told "no" before are eligible today. If you've applied in the past and been denied, the answer has likely changed.

## What you get

Up to about $292 per month for groceries, loaded onto a CalFresh EBT card you can use at most grocery stores, farmers' markets, and many corner stores. CalFresh does **not** affect your financial aid, does **not** count as income on your taxes, and does **not** have to be paid back. It's a federal benefit you've already paid into.

## How to apply with Civica

Civica is a free app built for CCC and CSU students. A trained navigator reviews your application and submits it for you, so you don't have to navigate BenefitsCal alone.

1. **Download the app.** Install via TestFlight: `[TestFlight link — Matthew to provide]`. Until the App Store launch, TestFlight is the only way in.
2. **Answer about 10 questions.** Takes ~10 minutes on your phone. The app asks about your school, income, and household.
3. **A navigator reviews and submits.** A Civica navigator checks your packet, asks for anything missing, and files it with the county on your behalf. You'll get a CalFresh decision letter (usually within 7 days for expedited cases, 30 days otherwise).

## What you need

Have these ready before you start — phone photos are fine:

- Photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport, or campus ID)
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease, or a letter from your school)
- Last 4 weeks of pay stubs (only if you work)
- Your current school enrollment status (a screenshot from your student portal works)
