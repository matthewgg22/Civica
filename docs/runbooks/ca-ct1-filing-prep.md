## What the state requires (confirmed on oag.ca.gov, 2026-08-25)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Form** | CT-1, Initial Registration |
| **Fee** | **$50**, credit card or ACH |
| **File at** | `https://ca-rcf.evokeplatform.com/app/registrationPortal` |
| **Deadline** | within **30 days of first receiving charitable assets** |

**Required attachments:**

1. Articles of Incorporation (and/or Articles of Organization, Trust Agreement)
2. Current **Bylaws**
3. **IRS determination letter**
4. **IRS Form 1023** (the exemption application as submitted)

## The good news for Civica Torrey specifically

**No Form 990 is required for initial registration.** The absence of a 990 and audited
financials has been treated throughout this work as a blocker — for *bank grant intake* it
genuinely is one, but it does **not** block CT-1. The instructions address the
not-yet-filed case directly: if the determination letter has not arrived, you supply the
FEIN; ours has arrived, so that is moot.

The determination came in **August 2026**, so the 1023 and the determination letter are
both recent and should be easy to locate.

## Before you file — three things to confirm

1. **Whether you have already received California charitable assets.** The 30-day clock
   runs from first receipt, not from incorporation. If nothing Californian has come in yet,
   you are not late; if something has, check the date against the deadline.
2. **Registration-before-solicitation.** I could not confirm on the AG's public pages
   whether California requires registration *before* soliciting or only within 30 days of
   receiving assets. Treat it as before-soliciting — that is the conservative reading and
   it is how the other states in this program work. Do not send a CA bank a pitch on the
   assumption that filing can follow.
3. **The registered agent and address of record.** Civica Torrey is MA-headquartered.
   Registering to solicit in California may carry a separate agent-for-service
   requirement; confirm at filing time.
