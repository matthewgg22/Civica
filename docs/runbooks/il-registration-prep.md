## Illinois is not California

California is one $50 online form. **Illinois is two filings in sequence, with a
Massachusetts document in front of both**, because Civica Torrey is MA-incorporated.

### Step 1 — Massachusetts: certified copy of the Articles

The Illinois Secretary of State requires **a certified copy of the Articles of
Incorporation and all amendments, issued within the last 90 days**. Order this from the
Massachusetts Secretary of the Commonwealth first; nothing downstream can proceed without
it, and it expires.

### Step 2 — Illinois Secretary of State: Form NFP 113.15

*Application for Authority to Conduct Affairs in Illinois* — foreign not-for-profit
qualification.

| | |
|---|---|
| Fee | **$50** (one secondary source says $25 — confirm at filing) |
| Submitted | **in duplicate** |
| Attachment | the certified MA Articles from step 1, dated within 90 days |
| Office | Corporations Division, 501 S. Second St., Room 351, Springfield IL 62756 · 217-524-8008 |

This yields the **Certificate of Authority**, which step 3 requires.

### Step 3 — Illinois Attorney General: Form CO-1

*Charitable Organization Registration Statement*, Charitable Trust Bureau.

| | |
|---|---|
| Registration fee | **$15.00** |
| Late registration fee | **$200.00** if the organization solicits before registering |
| Annual report | AG990-IL, **$15.00** each · **$100.00** late fee each |

**Required with the CO-1:**

1. Form CO-1
2. List of all Officers, Directors and Trustees — names, mailing addresses, **daytime phone numbers**
3. IRS Determination Letter (ours, August 2026)
4. Articles of Incorporation and all amendments, **plus the Certificate of Authority from step 2**, plus Bylaws
5. Financial history — see below

## The financial-history question, which is the good news

Item 11 of the AG's checklist requires **Form AG990-IL for each of the past 3 years in
which the organization held or solicited charitable funds in Illinois** — except:

> *"If the organization has been in existence for less than 1 year, submit the Form CO-2."*

**Form CO-2 is the path for a new organization.** As in California, the absence of a filed
990 is not a blocker here — but the test is **existence**, not determination. Our
determination is August 2026; **incorporation may be earlier**. Check the incorporation
date against the one-year line before assuming CO-2 applies. If Civica Torrey has been in
existence more than a year, AG990-ILs are required for the years it held or solicited
Illinois funds — and if it held none, that is a much shorter conversation with the Bureau.

## Register before soliciting — settled, unlike California

Illinois answers directly what California's pages would not: there is a **$200 late
registration fee "for any organization that solicits contributions"** before registering.
Register first. Approaching Busey, First American or Lakeside ahead of registration costs
$200 and starts the relationship in violation.
