# Massachusetts charitable registration — prep

**Prepared 2026-08-25.** Preparation only; the filings are signed and submitted by an officer.

## What it gates

The entire Massachusetts mortgage-lender channel — **99 lenders, 38 of them rated Needs to
Improve on the state Service Test**, who wrote **11,213 Massachusetts mortgages in 2024**.
That is the densest documented pressure anywhere in the pipeline: about 67% carrying a gap
against roughly 6% among banks nationally.

It also gates any Massachusetts bank we later add.

## Massachusetts is the easy one, because it is home

Civica Torrey is Massachusetts-headquartered. Unlike Illinois — which needs a certified
copy of the Articles from Massachusetts, then a foreign-qualification filing with the
Secretary of State, then the Attorney General — **Massachusetts requires no foreign
qualification at all.** There is no out-of-state document in front of it and no 90-day
clock to manage.

### 1. Initial registration — Non-Profit Organizations / Public Charities Division

|---|---|
| Fee | **$100** |

| Office | Office of the Attorney General, Non-Profit Organizations / Public Charities Division |
| Contact | **(617) 963-2101** · **Charities@Mass.gov** |
| Filed via | the Attorney General's Charity Portal |

### 2. Schedule A-2 — Certificate of Solicitation

**This is the piece that matters for us.** A registered charity that wants to begin
soliciting *before its first Form PC is due* files **Schedule A-2 with a $50 payment** in
the Charity Portal, as an application for a Certificate of Solicitation.

That is precisely Civica Torrey's situation: registered, newly determined, no Form PC yet,
and wanting to solicit now. The absence of a filed return is not an obstacle here — it is
the case the mechanism exists for.

**Total to solicit: $150.** That is the $100 + $50 our notes recorded, now resolved into
two distinct filings rather than one fee.

## The annual obligation, and why it is cheap for now

**Form PC** is the annual report. Its fee is banded by revenue, **$35 to $2,000**, so a
first year at minimal revenue sits at the bottom of that range. Schedules A-1 and A-2
attach to it whenever funds are raised from the public.

Two audit thresholds are worth knowing before the grants land:

- revenue over **$200,000** → a **reviewed** financial statement must accompany Form PC
- revenue over **$500,000** → a **full audit** must accompany Form PC

The current roster of prepared asks totals $293,500. **If the bank channel succeeds
beyond roughly $200,000 in a year, Massachusetts will require a reviewed financial
statement** — a real cost that should be budgeted at the point the pipeline starts
converting, not discovered afterwards.

## Confirm at filing

1. **Whether registration must precede solicitation.** Illinois settles this with a $200
   late fee for soliciting first; Massachusetts's own timing language was not readable
   here (see below). The Certificate of Solicitation mechanism strongly implies
   *register, then get the certificate, then solicit* — treat it that way.
2. **The exact initial-registration form number.** Form PC is the annual return; the
   initial registration is a separate portal submission and its designation should be
   taken from the portal itself.

## A retrieval note

Every mass.gov page and document in this work returns **403 to scripted clients**, and the
domain is blocked in the in-app browser, so none of the above could be read from the
source directly — it comes from search summaries and secondary compliance guides.
**Verify the fees and forms in the Charity Portal before paying anything.** The same block
is why the 38 lender evaluations remain unread; they open normally in an ordinary browser.

## The three states side by side

| | Path | Cash | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MA** | registration $100 → Schedule A-2 $50 · no foreign qualification | **$150** | 99 lenders incl. 38 at Needs to Improve |
| **CA** | one CT-1, online, no 990 needed | **$50** | 5 banks, $91,500 |
| **IL** | MA certified Articles → SOS NFP 113.15 → AG CO-1 | **~$65** + MA copy | 3 banks, $49,500 |

California is still the cheapest single move. **Massachusetts is the cheapest per prospect
by a wide margin** — $150 opens 99 institutions — and, being the home state, it is the only
one of the three with no sequencing risk.
