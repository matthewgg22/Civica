# Arizona, Texas, New York, Tennessee — registration prep

**Prepared 2026-08-25.** Preparation only; any filing is signed and submitted by an officer.

These were the last four unresearched states, holding **$65,500** of prepared asks. Two of
them require **no registration at all**, and the other two are trivial.

## Arizona — nothing to file

**$25,000 · Western Alliance Bank · Maricopa + Pinal**

Arizona **repealed** its charitable solicitation registration requirement. House Bill 2457
struck former A.R.S. §44-6552, and as of **13 September 2013 the Secretary of State stopped
accepting registrations** from charities soliciting in Arizona.

Two exceptions, neither ours: **veterans' organizations** still register, and **professional
fundraisers** hired by a charity must register before running a campaign on its behalf. If
we solicit Western Alliance ourselves, nothing is required.

**Cost: $0. Nothing to file. Not blocked.**

## Texas — nothing to file

**$19,000 · Texas First Bank $12,500 · Third Coast Bank $6,500 · Harris County**

Texas has **no general charitable solicitation registration law**. Only three categories
must register: **law enforcement organisations soliciting by telephone**, **public safety
organisations**, and **veterans organisations**, each with their solicitors. We are none.

One caveat that is not solicitation registration: an organisation that maintains offices,
employees or other in-state operations may need to register to *do business* in Texas.
Soliciting a bank by post, phone or email is not that, but if Civica ever puts staff or an
office in Texas the question changes.

**Cost: $0. Nothing to file. Not blocked.**

## New York — $25, and our note was wrong

**$15,000 · Habib American Bank (trading as HAB Bank) · Kings County**

**Form CHAR410**, filed online after creating an account with the Attorney General's
Charities Bureau. **Fee $25.**

Required: EIN · organisational structure and charitable purposes · officers, directors,
trustees and key employees · **IRS Form 1023 or 1024** · certificate of incorporation ·
bylaws.

**This project has been carrying "NY CHAR410 — known onerous" since early on. That is
wrong about the initial registration**, which is $25 and unremarkable. New York's
reputation comes from the **annual CHAR500** and its audit thresholds, which arrive later
and only once revenue exists. Do not let the note discourage the filing.

Note also: because CHAR410 asks for the **1023**, keep that document to hand — Illinois,
Massachusetts and Pennsylvania all want it too.

## Tennessee — $50, and probably $0

**$6,500 · FirstBank · Davidson County (Nashville)**

**Form SS-6001**, Division of Charitable Solicitations and Gaming, Secretary of State.
**Fee $50.**

But there is an exemption that almost certainly applies: an organisation that does not
intend to solicit, and does not actually receive, **more than $50,000 in gross
contributions from the public** in a fiscal year files an **Exemption Request instead** —
and **exempt organisations are charged no fee**.

The exemption is **annual**, so it must be refiled each year, and it is defined on gross
public contributions rather than on the size of any single ask. Confirm which side of
$50,000 Civica expects to land on before choosing the path.

**Cost: $0 if exempt, $50 if not.**
