# CRA Officer Call Guide

The T7 artifact (design doc D3.5). One page of preparation per call; the calls are the entire payoff of the 3-week loop — don't wing the shots. Written 2026-08-22, incorporating the second-pass research (bank-giving diligence norms + CRA procedural requirements, both in [cra-pilot-research-2026-08-22.md](cra-pilot-research-2026-08-22.md)).

## The one-sentence frame

"We generate documented, exam-ready community-development activity — SNAP enrollment outreach for provably-LMI residents of your assessment area — and we deliver the qualification memo with the grant, not months later on request."

## Ammunition (verified citations — use them, sparingly)

- **SNAP is a named LMI proxy.** Interagency Q&A §__.12(g)(2)—1 expressly lists "recipients of government assistance programs that have income qualifications equivalent to, or stricter than" LMI, naming "U.S. Department of Agriculture's… Supplemental Nutrition Assistance programs." Their examiner does not have to interpret anything — the proxy is verbatim in the guidance their exam runs on.
- **Grants are qualified investments.** Q&A §__.12(t)—5: charitable contributions, cash or in-kind, are "grants"; tax deduction doesn't disqualify.
- **Documentation burden is theirs — we remove it.** Q&A §__.12(h)—8: "institutions… should be prepared to demonstrate the activities' qualifications." Our packet (memo + annual impact letter, county-level geography) is exam-file-ready on day one.
- **A binding multi-year pledge counts NOW.** Q&A §__.23(e)—2: legally binding up-front commitments booked under GAAP count in full at commitment, and outstanding balances keep counting at later exams (§__.12(t)—8: prior-period investments with multi-year impact can outweigh same-size current ones). → A 2-year binding pledge signed before their exam is worth more than two annual checks.
- **Program dollars, not galas.** Examiners commonly disallow event-sponsorship portions spent on catering/venue. All our dollars are program delivery — say so.
- **ISB nuance:** for intermediate small banks ($~350M–$1.4B), "innovativeness" is NOT a factor (Q&A §__.21(a)—2) — volume, responsiveness, and documentation are. Don't oversell novelty to an ISB; sell clean paper.

## The questions (in order; the loop's open items each have an owner-question)

1. **AA confirmation:** "Your most recent evaluation delineates [counties]. Is that still current — any changes coming before the next exam?" *(Also catches never-examined new AAs — Helm/Ocean's Orlando — which are clean-slate opportunities.)*
2. **Precision vs. effort (decides the pitch):** "When your team documents community-development grants for the exam file, what's the hard part — proving LMI benefit and geography, or just the time it takes? Has an examiner ever pushed back on a grant's qualification?"
3. **Budget & vehicle:** "Does support like this fit your CRA/community-development grant budget, a sponsorship/marketing budget, or the foundation? What are the cycles for each — could something land this quarter?" *(Grant cycles are often annual; sponsorship budgets move in weeks.)*
4. **Timing to exam:** "Your next evaluation window is [quarter]. Is there activity you'd want booked before then? Would a binding multi-year commitment be more useful than a single-year check?" *(Q&A §__.23(e)—2 makes the pledge count at commitment.)*
5. **Diligence & the audit question (NEW — tests the CEO-plan hypothesis):** "What financial documentation does your process require from a grantee — and is any audited-financials requirement keyed to the grant size or to our organization's operating budget? We're first-year: determination letter, board list, and a current-year budget in lieu of a 990 — is that workable, and at what number does it stop being workable?" *(Research: no major bank publishes a grant-size trigger; BofA's published line is org budget ≥ $250K → audit. Record each bank's actual answer — this is the data the mid-band pricing rule depends on.)*
6. **The BDT question (converts our weakness into their unresolved problem):** "Benefits Data Trust shut down in 2024 — some banks funded exactly this work through them. Did you? What would make a successor fundable for you?"
7. **The close (kill-criterion 3 discipline):** "What would a concrete next step look like — a proposal to a named budget line, a second conversation with [decision-maker], or written conditions we should meet?" *(No concrete next step within 10 days of the meeting = a no. Log it in the tracking sheet either way.)*

## Per-bank openers

- **NTI banks (Helm, Banco do Brasil, Pearland, Bank Irvine, Tier B):** "Your next evaluation is the one that matters. We produce documented, citable community-services activity in your assessment area on a quarterly cadence — and the paperwork arrives with the check."
- **Exam-scheduled Satisfactory banks (Ocean, Apple, Stock Yards, IBC…):** lead with the never-examined-AA or footprint-need number, then question 4.
- **Merger-pending (Helm again, Dallas Capital, BancFirst):** "Merger review looks at your CRA record. Activity booked now is in the file the Fed reads."
- **Consent-order banks (City National LA):** enter through compliance/community-development, not the foundation: "Your order's outreach and partnership commitments have a clock. We're a turnkey line item with LMI documentation your monitor will accept."

## Financial posture (answers we give when asked)

- First-year 501(c)(3), determination Aug 2026. Packet: determination letter, officer/board list, current-FY budget (the standard budget-in-lieu-of-990 path that BofA-class programs explicitly accept), program budget.
- **CPA review budget note:** no statutory audit applies to us (MA review threshold $500K / audit $1M; CA audit $2M; FL review $500K / audit $1M — all far above current revenue). If a funder wants more than the budget packet, or if first-year revenue approaches ~$250K (the only published bank trigger), we will commission a **CPA review (~$5K, budgeted)** at fiscal year-end — a voluntary credibility purchase, priced into the program budget, engaged ~90 days ahead. Say this proactively when question 5 gets a hesitant answer; it converts "no audit" from a gap into a plan.
- Pricing rule (unchanged): asks stay mid-band ($5–50K by bank size); never structure compensation per enrollment.

## After every call

Log in the tracking sheet: bank, path, questions 2/3/4/5 answers verbatim-ish, next step + date, kill-criterion-3 clock start. Send the thank-you email same day with the one-page CRA qualification memo attached — the memo IS the follow-up.
