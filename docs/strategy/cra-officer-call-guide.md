# CRA Officer Call Guide (v2 — PE-informed)

Rewritten 2026-08-22 after reading all seven published Performance Evaluations for the target banks ([cra-pe-giving-intelligence-2026-08-22.md](cra-pe-giving-intelligence-2026-08-22.md)). v1 guessed at what banks want; this version quotes what their own examiners wrote about them.

**The core move:** every bank on this list has a *documented, published* weakness in its evaluation — in the examiner's own words. We are not pitching a good cause. We are answering, specifically and in their vocabulary, the criticism their regulator already put in writing.

---

## The frame

> "Your last evaluation says [verbatim criticism]. We deliver community services to low- and moderate-income individuals in [their AA] — with the qualification memo and quarterly documentation written for your exam file, so the activity is countable the day it happens."

Three sentences. The first is theirs, not ours. That's what makes it land.

## What the PEs changed about our pitch

1. **Frame as community services + financial capability — never "food security."** Across seven PEs and a dozen community contacts, **no examiner and no contact ever named food security, SNAP, or benefits access as a need.** They named affordable housing, LMI mortgage products, **financial literacy/capability**, and small-business technical assistance. That is also exactly where the grant dollars flow (Community Services took 18 of 19 of Helm's grants, 48 of 60 at Banco do Brasil, 84% of Ocean's grant dollars, $1.3M of Bank of Marin's). Speak their category.
2. **The credit research is the natural bridge — but cite the paper that exists.** The evidence runs in the *loss* direction, and that is the honest, strong version: Dodini, Larrimore & Tranfaglia, "Financial Repercussions of SNAP Work Requirements" (Federal Reserve Board FEDS 2022-030; *Journal of Public Economics* 229, 2024) used the FRBNY/Equifax Consumer Credit Panel and found that when households lose SNAP access, credit inquiries rise ~12.9%, card balances rise ~$132 (+7.9%), the probability of a past-due card rises, and **credit application success rates fall** — distress borrowing, not improved creditworthiness. Say it as: *"When people lose SNAP, the Fed's own research shows they substitute into unsecured credit and their approval rates fall. Enrollment assistance addresses that substitution at its source."* Pair with the burden evidence — Giannella, Homonoff, Rino & Somerville (NBER WP 31239) found flexible interview scheduling raised SNAP approvals **+6 percentage points** across 65,000 LA County applicants — which is evidence that *our actual intervention* works. **Never claim a credit-score point gain from SNAP enrollment: no such estimate exists in the literature.** See [cra-research-citations-2026-08-22.md](cra-research-citations-2026-08-22.md) for the do-not-cite list.

3. **A grant does NOT fix a Needs-to-Improve rating — never imply it does.** Both NTI banks failed the *Lending* test. Helm's CD Test was **Satisfactory**; Banco do Brasil's Investment Test was **High Satisfactory**. What is true and sufficient: they cannot easily change their lending model, so community development is the lever they *can* move, and both have specific CD-side gaps on record (below).
4. **Ask at their disclosed giving level, not their asset size.** Grants are 0.03%–3% of CD investment dollars at these banks; the money is in securities. Asking above a bank's historical grant ceiling signals you didn't read the file.
5. **Paid delivery counts — cite the precedent.** Five Star's examiners called a bank-funded contract counselor *"especially innovative."* This supports "fund navigator capacity," not "send your staff to volunteer."

## Battle cards — their documented gap, our answer, the ask

Each quote below is verbatim from that bank's own Performance Evaluation.

### Ocean Bank — ask $25,000 · Q3 2026 exam · the sharpest opening in the set
- **Their gap:** the Fort Lauderdale/Broward AA received **zero grants and zero CD services** benefiting only that AA. Examiners: *"the high percentage of low- and moderate-income families, at 39.6 percent, and the poverty rate indicate needs for financial literacy and affordable housing."* Investment Test is Low Satisfactory — *"adequate… although rarely in a leadership position,"* volume *"below four similarly situated banks,"* services *"below all but one institution."*
- **Our answer:** a Broward-dedicated program. ~169,000 Broward residents are eligible-unenrolled. This converts a documented zero into a documented program in the exact AA and the exact need category the examiner named.
- **Opener:** "Your Broward assessment area recorded no community development services and no donations last cycle, and your examiner tied Broward's 39.6% LMI share to a financial-literacy need. We can put a documented program there before your next exam."

### Bank of Marin — ask $10,000 · Q3 2026 exam
- **Their gap:** Low Satisfactory on **all three tests**. *"All of the new qualified investments are mortgage-backed securities, which are not innovative or complex."* **Zero** community-services investments — every non-housing dollar came through the grant line. CD service hours **fell 17%** and are *"below the ratio range of similarly situated institutions."* Their Sacramento examiner listed opportunities including *"business technical assistance, and financial literacy education."*
- **Our answer:** a community-services activity that is neither an MBS nor an employee-hours program — the one gap their portfolio structurally cannot fill with securities.
- **Opener:** "Your investment book is entirely affordable-housing MBS, which your examiner noted isn't innovative or complex, and your service hours dropped 17%. We're a community-services program in your AA that documents itself."

### Hanmi Bank — ask $15,000 (hard ceiling) · Q3 2026 exam
- **Their gap:** Investment Test Low Satisfactory, *"rarely in a leadership position,"* *"rarely uses innovative and/or complex investments."* Giving is **declining** — LA fell from $519,840 (52 grants) to $281,080 (14 grants). Already places 3,116 of 3,496 CD service hours in Community Services.
- **Our answer:** they're already committed to the Community Services category; we add documented volume there at a price inside their ceiling ($15,000 is the largest grant appearing anywhere in their PE).
- **Note the pattern:** NTI in 2021 → spent → Satisfactory 2023 → cut giving. With an exam now, that number should be climbing again.

### Banco do Brasil Americas — ask $15,000 · Q3 2026 re-exam · already funds our peers
- **Their gap:** NTI overall (Lending). Investment Test High Satisfactory and grants grew **6.3×**. **Orlando AA recorded ONE community development service** all period, called a *"limited"* level.
- **Our answer:** they already fund this exact work — six donations totaling **$20,500** to a nonprofit providing *"crisis care, food and household subsidy, and employment training,"* and five totaling **$10,000** to one providing *"emergency food and shelter, rent and utility payment assistance, and financial literacy education."* We are that program, with documentation attached and Orlando coverage.
- **Opener:** "You've funded crisis-care and financial-literacy organizations at $10–20K. We do that work with exam-ready documentation, and we can cover Orlando, where your last evaluation recorded a single community development service."

### Helm Bank USA — ask $7,500 (sponsor tier) · Q4 2026 re-exam
- **Their gap:** CD services *"below the performance of similarly situated institutions."* Their community contact: *"a significant need for … financial literacy education to low- and moderate-income individuals."*
- **Reality check:** entire grant program = **$25,161 over 3.2 years, ~$1,300 average**. Ask the sponsor tier and mean it. Their CD Test already passed; the honest sell is the services gap, not rating rescue.

### American Business Bank — ask $50,000 · Q4 2026 exam · biggest realistic check
- **Their posture:** High Satisfactory; grants **+185.7% by dollar**, *"doubled the grants at one comparable bank and tripled the grants at the other."* Demonstrated **$458,000** to a single CDC. Employees already do **free income tax preparation for low-income families** — the closest analogue in the entire set to benefits-application assistance.
- **Their gap:** CD services *"much below the other comparable bank that was a leader,"* and investment ratios *"decreased since the excellent performance at the prior evaluation."*
- **Opener:** "Your team already runs free tax prep for low-income families — this is the same population and the same moment, at a scale volunteering can't reach."

### Five Star Bank — ask $30,000 · Q4 2026 exam
- **Their posture:** Investment Test High Satisfactory ("excellent" in Sacramento); wrote **$900,000** to one Title I school. Service Test **Low Satisfactory** — *"no branch in low-, one branch in moderate-"* income tracts. Chico contact: *"poverty and homelessness, unemployment, and a lack of knowledge for financial resources… more can be done."*
- **Our answer:** a service-side program reaching LMI tracts their branch network doesn't. **Cite their own precedent:** their contracted counselor was called *"especially innovative"* — bank-funded professional delivery, exactly what they'd be funding here.

### Bank Irvine — ask $10,000 · long shot, honestly
- Examined under **Small Bank procedures**: streamlined lending test, CD activity optional and counting only toward an *Outstanding*. Its NTI is a lending-distribution failure a grant cannot repair. Strongest pressure signal, weakest structural fit. Pitch as sponsorship; expect a no.

## The questions (in order)

1. **AA confirmation:** "Your evaluation delineates [counties]. Still current before the next exam?" *(Catches Helm's and Ocean's never-examined new AAs — clean-slate opportunities.)*
2. **The gap, asked as a question, not an accusation:** "Your last evaluation flagged [their verbatim item]. Is that what your team is working against this cycle, or has the priority moved?"
3. **Budget & vehicle:** "Does this fit the CRA/community-development grant budget, a sponsorship line, or the foundation — and what are the cycles for each? Could something land this quarter?"
4. **Exam timing + the pledge play:** "Your window is [quarter]. Would a binding two-year commitment be more useful than a single-year check?" *(A legally binding pledge counts in full at commitment — Q&A §__.23(e)—2 — so two years of credit lands before the exam at the same annual cost.)*
5. **Diligence / the audit trigger:** "What financial documentation does your process require — and is any audited-financials requirement keyed to grant size or to our operating budget? We're first-year: determination letter, board list, current-year budget in lieu of a 990." *(Research: no major bank publishes a grant-size trigger; BofA's published line is org budget ≥ $250K. Record each bank's actual answer.)*
6. **The BDT question:** "Benefits Data Trust shut down in 2024 — some banks funded this work through them. Did you? What would make a successor fundable for you?"
7. **The close (kill criterion 3):** "What's a concrete next step — a proposal to a named budget line, a second conversation with [decision-maker], or written conditions?" *No concrete next step within 10 days = a no.*

## Ammunition (plain English, citation in the pocket)

- **The rulebook already says SNAP counts.** The examiner FAQ lists acceptable proof that a grant helps LMI people, and "people who receive SNAP" is literally an example on the list. You never argue your users are low-income; the bank points at the list. *(Q&A §__.12(g)(2)—1, naming USDA's Supplemental Nutrition Assistance programs.)*
- **Grants count as qualified investments** — cash or in-kind, and the tax deduction doesn't disqualify. *(§__.12(t)—5.)*
- **A two-year promise counts the day it's signed** and keeps counting at later exams. *(§__.23(e)—2; prior-period credit §__.12(t)—8.)*
- **The paperwork burden is theirs — we do their homework.** At exam they must prove each grant qualified: who got it, that beneficiaries were LMI, that it happened in their territory. Most nonprofits send a thank-you note; we deliver the completed exam-file memo with the check. A company (findCRA) exists solely to sell banks these memos. *(§__.12(h)—8.)*
- **Every dollar counts — no gala haircut.** Examiners subtract the catering-and-ballroom share of event sponsorships; ours is 100% program delivery.
- **Mid-size banks buy paperwork, not novelty.** For intermediate small banks (~$350M–$1.4B) "innovativeness" earns nothing at their test — sell clean paper and reliable quarterly numbers. *(§__.21(a)—2.)*
- **Need data raises responsiveness weighting — it does not "earn credit."** Examiners are directed to consider bank-supplied information about community development needs; responsiveness to identified needs is scored on every test. Offer our county figures as input to *their* performance-context narrative.

## Financial posture (when asked)

First-year 501(c)(3), determination August 2026. Packet: determination letter, officer/board list, current-FY budget (the budget-in-lieu-of-990 path major programs explicitly accept), program budget. **No statutory audit applies** (MA review $500K/audit $1M; CA audit $2M; FL review $500K/audit $1M — all far above us). If a funder wants more, or first-year revenue approaches ~$250K, we commission a **CPA review (~$5K, budgeted)** at year-end, engaged ~90 days ahead. Say this proactively when question 5 draws a hesitant answer — it turns "no audit" into a plan.

**Where each dollar goes** (same split at every bank): 60¢ outreach media in their AA · 15¢ measurement and reporting · 10¢ creative and Spanish localization · **15¢ indirect costs**. Say the indirect line as: *"Indirect is budgeted at 15% — the federal de minimis rate at 2 CFR 200.414(f), raised from 10% in the 2024 Uniform Guidance revisions — which is below the 25% median Bridgespan documents for direct-service nonprofits."* Have the line-item build (bookkeeping, D&O and cyber insurance, three state charitable registrations, CPA review, legal) ready as an attachment.

## After every call

Log: bank, path, verbatim answers to questions 2–5, next step + date, kill-criterion-3 clock. Same-day thank-you with the **specimen qualification memo attached** — the memo is the follow-up.
