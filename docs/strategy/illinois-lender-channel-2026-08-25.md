# The Illinois lender channel does not exist yet

**Date:** 2026-08-25 · Source: IDFPR's own ILCRA pages, read in-browser (the pages are
JS-rendered and empty to curl)

## The finding

I described 162 Illinois mortgage lenders as "the largest unworked list in the project."
That was wrong in an important way. **The list is unworked because there is nothing to work
yet.**

IDFPR's Mortgage Lenders Public Evaluations page reads, in full:

> **INFORMATION COMING SOON!**

with an empty 2025 table beneath it. **Illinois has published zero mortgage-lender CRA
performance evaluations.**

The regime is real — 205 ILCS 735, Division of Banking, four ratings from outstanding to
substantial noncompliance under §35-15(c), an examination template already drafted. But no
lender has been rated. The entire Massachusetts pitch rests on *"your Service Test is rated
Needs to Improve,"* and in Illinois there are no ratings to point at.

**A 162-name list with no ratings behind it is a mailing list, not a pipeline.**

## Two things worth having anyway

### The first ILCRA mortgage exams ever, both this quarter

The upcoming-schedule page names exactly two lenders:

| Lender | Location | Exam |
|---|---|---|
| **American Portfolio Mortgage** | Rolling Meadows | Q3 2026 |
| **Success Lending** | Schaumburg | Q3 2026 |

These are the **first mortgage-lender examinations under the Illinois CRA**, happening now.
Both face an examiner with no prior record to be measured against and no established
benchmark. That is a genuinely different pitch from remediation — not *"fix a documented
failure"* but *"have something to show the first time anyone looks."*

Small, specific, and time-bound. Worth a call before the quarter closes.

### Parkway Bank and Trust is examined by Illinois in Q4 2026

Parkway appears on the **Fourth Quarter 2026 bank examination schedule**.

We already read Parkway's federal evaluation: 16 Cook County branches, $27.1 million of
investments in its Illinois assessment area, and **$7,000 of donations** — which priced it
at $500, a deep pool figure. On the federal record alone it was not worth pursuing.

**A second regulator examining it within months changes the timing, not the arithmetic.**
The ask stays small because their giving is small. But a bank with a $7,000 donation record
walking into a state exam has a reason to listen that its federal PE did not supply.

## The Illinois bank programme has no pressure in it

Twelve bank evaluations published across 2025–2026, every one **Satisfactory or
Outstanding**, all small community institutions. None of our three Illinois targets — Busey,
First American, Lakeside — appears in either the published list or the schedule.

So Illinois state CRA adds no pressure signal to the bank side either, beyond Parkway's
timing.

## What this means for the coverage gaps

The previous coverage note listed Illinois lenders first, as the largest and most
promising omission. **Demote it.** The correct order is now:

1. **New York lenders (70)** — NYDFS regime; verify it has actually published evaluations
   before repeating this mistake.
2. **Credit unions** under MA/CT/RI.
3. **Illinois lenders** — revisit when IDFPR starts publishing. Watch the
   mortgage-lenders-public-evaluations page.

## Method note

Both IDFPR pages are JS-rendered. `curl` returns 200 with zero PDF links and no table, which
would read as "no data" rather than "not rendered." The browser showed the real content.
**A 200 with empty content is not evidence of absence** — the same trap as the OCC's
Google-search-in-a-page.
