# The Massachusetts lender channel

**Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Worklist:** `state-cra-mortgage/ma_lender_worklist_2026.csv` (99 lenders)

## Why this channel is different

**38 of 57 scanned Massachusetts mortgage lenders carry a Service Test rated Needs to
Improve** — about 67%. Across banks in the national pressure sweep the rate is roughly 6%.

That is a regime difference, not noise. Massachusetts examines non-depository mortgage
lenders under its own state CRA (M.G.L. c.255E §8), and the service-delivery component is
one many of them barely address. The examiner says so in writing, and every one of those
evaluations is public.

## I could not read them, and that is a retrieval problem

The evaluations live on mass.gov. It refuses scripted clients with a 403 — the same WAF
behaviour hit earlier in this work — and the domain is blocked by policy in the in-app
browser. Both routes are closed to me.

**They open normally in an ordinary browser.** Every PE URL is in the worklist, so reading
one is a click. That is the only reason this channel is unpriced.

## The pitch here is not the bank pitch

For a bank, the ask is anchored on disclosed giving in a named assessment area, because
that is the unit the examiner scores. **Mortgage lenders are not scored that way.** They
have no assessment area in the bank sense and no Investment Test; what they have is a
Service Test measuring whether they reach LMI borrowers across Massachusetts.

So the anchoring method built for banks does not transfer, and pricing these will need its
own rule — most likely built from Massachusetts origination volume rather than disclosed
giving. Do not reuse the 15%-of-assessment-area-giving formula here without deciding that
it applies.

and it is unfiled. That is the whole channel behind one form.
