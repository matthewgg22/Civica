## Umpqua is the second phantom

Its evaluation mentions **Arizona zero times. Phoenix zero times. Maricopa zero times.** Its
rated areas are Washington, Oregon, California, Idaho, Nevada and the Lewiston Multistate MSA.
The ranking placed it at "5 branches, Maricopa AZ" on current FDIC branch data.

Note also that **cert 17266 is now "Columbia Bank"** — Umpqua merged into it, and the December
2023 evaluation still carries the Umpqua name. Same pattern as German American: **branch data
is current, evaluations are historical.** That is now two phantom targets from one ranking, and
the failure mode is confirmed rather than anecdotal.

## The wrong-AA trap, caught a fourth time

IBC's evaluation states, in narrative: *"The bank made use of 279 qualified donations totaling
$1.2 million within the assessment area."* That is a clean, AA-scoped donations sentence — of
exactly the shape we have been hunting.

**It belongs to the LAREDO MSA assessment area.** The paragraph opens *"IBC Laredo demonstrated
a needs to improve record under the Investment Test within the Laredo MSA AA."* Houston is a
different, limited-scope area in the same document.

Houston's actual row is 59 items totaling $6,771 thousand, combining investments and donations,
and **$6,498 thousand of it is a single affordable-housing item.** The residue is plausibly
donations — the Laredo row's 279 items are all donations — but *plausibly* is not a figure to
quote at a bank, so it is not recorded as one.

## Western State

The Arizona rated area **is** the Phoenix assessment area — one AA covering Maricopa and Pinal.
It totals 67 items / $1,417 thousand, combined. Neither the rated-area table nor the
assessment-area table carries a donations row for Arizona; the latter breaks out only the North
Dakota areas. Its only gap is on **Investment**, which this project deliberately does not treat
as a targeting signal.

## What the batch confirms

The probe's ordering held. Of nine banks it ranked, the two it flagged most strongly
(Republic, Enterprise) both loaded, the one flagged as a possible false negative (Citizens
Business) loaded and turned out to be the **second-largest peer capacity on the roster**, and
the three it ranked lowest all failed — two on missing figures and one on having no assessment
area at all.

**Twelve banks have now been read.** A donations-only assessment-area figure exists in **seven**.
It is present when the area is full-scope *and* the bank publishes a Qualified Grants &
Donations row; absent otherwise. No amount of additional looking changes a document that does
not contain the number.
