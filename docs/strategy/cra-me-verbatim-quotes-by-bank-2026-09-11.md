## County quote-bank (each county once)

### Sacramento — FFY2024 *(Five Star Bank)*
- **Access verdict** (l.1336): "The county does not provide appropriate access to potential CF applicants."
- **Expedited service** (l.1410): "The county is not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing."
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.306): "…the appointment letter was sent in Vietnamese when the applicants' preferred written language was English."
- **Wrong-language + no GEN 1365** (l.~ "preferred written language was Farsi"): "…the applicant's preferred written language was Farsi. The form was not accompanied with the Notice of Language Services (GEN 1365). (MPP 21-115; ACL 17-102)"
- **Confidentiality** (l.50): "Confidentiality was not consistently maintained."
- **Over-verification** (l.288): "…the CW 2200 form over verified information by requesting for 'proof of birth' which is not required for the CF program and United States (U.S.) citizenship was not documented as questionable."

### Yolo — FFY2025 *(Five Star Bank)*
- **Expedited service** (l.1095): "The county is not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing. On May 27, 2022, the county was placed on a Timeliness Corrective Action Plan (CAP) for three-day ES Application Processing Timeliness (APT)."
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.302): "…the request was in English instead of the household's preferred language of Spanish."
- **Confidentiality** (l.47): "Confidentiality was not consistently maintained. The CF program was disclosed before callers were authenticated with at least two items of personally identifiable information."
- **Over-verification** (l.276): "…the CW 2200 form requested for immunization records when it is not required for the CF program."

### Yuba — FFY2025 *(Five Star Bank — the benchmark/positive county)*
- **Access verdict (positive)** (l.1033): "The county provides appropriate access to potential CF applicants." *(Cite as the achievable ceiling in the AA, not a gap.)*
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.245): "…not [sent] in the household's preferred language."
- **Over-verification of a categorically-eligible household** (l.261): "…the CW 2200 form requested checking account verification for the Categorically Eligible household…" *(categorically-eligible households are not asset-tested).*

### Los Angeles — FFY2024 *(American Business Bank, City National, Bank Irvine, Hanmi, Mega)*
- **Wrong-language + over-verification (marquee)** (l.58): "CW 2200 forms did not request the last 30 days of income, limited the type of verification, requested information that was not required, and/or were not sent in the correct language."
- **Recommendation** (l.491): "Ensure that notices sent to households are in their preferred language."
- **Phone access** (l.426): "Callers experienced wait times ranging from 39 minutes to 1 hour and 22 minutes to [reach a representative]." *(Aug/Sept review: "…39 minutes to 1 hour and 15 minutes.")*

### Orange — FFY2024 *(American Business Bank, City National, Bank Irvine, Hanmi, Mega)*
- **Over-verification** (l.698): "CW 2200 form over verified information by requesting proof of citizenship and school [attendance]…"
- **Over-verification / single type** (l.273): "…the CW 2200 form limited the type of [acceptable verification]."
- **Confusing denial reason** (l.217): "…[the county informed the] household they were denied due to student ineligibility when it should have been for [non-citizen status]." *(Note: Orange's language staffing is strong — frame Orange as over-verification/notice-clarity, not a language gap.)*

### San Bernardino — FFY2025 *(American Business Bank, City National)*
- **Access verdict** (l.1051): "The county does not provide appropriate access to potential CF applicants."
- **Phone access** (l.1037): "The average wait time was 46 minutes. Some callers were not able to speak to a live [person]." · (l.52) "Mystery callers were not able to contact a live person."
- **Expedited service** (l.1140): "…not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing. On May 27, 2022, San Bernardino County was notified of the requirement to develop, submit, and implement a Corrective Action Plan (CAP)…"
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.233): "…the CW 2200 form was sent to the household in English when their preferred written language was Spanish."

### Riverside — FFY2024 *(American Business Bank, City National)*
- **Confidentiality** (l.745): "Confidentiality was not consistently maintained at the Coachella and Indio Offices. The CF program was disclosed before households were authenticated with at least two items of personally identifiable information (PII). (MPP 19-002.1 and 63-201.34)"
- **Over-verification / proof of a negative** (l.774): "CW 2200 form over verified information by requesting 'proof you do not receive meals at CVRM,' which is not required for CF. (MPP 63-402.4; ACL 19-51)"

### Riverside — FFY2025 *(American Business Bank, City National)*
- **Interview method / procedural** (l.222): "…[the household] requested their interview to be in person, but it was scheduled to be by telephone."
- **Expedited service mis-screen** (l.282): "…the Expedited Service (ES)-entitled household was not [screened correctly]…"
- **Extra county form** (l.292): "…the signature obtained on the 'DPSS 512B' county [form]…" *(a non-required form adding friction).*

### Ventura — FFY2024 *(City National)*
- **Expedited service** (l.1258): "The county is not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing. On September 21, 2023, the county was placed on a Timeliness Corrective Action Plan (CAP)…"
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.232): "…the denial NOA was sent to the household in Vietnamese when their preferred written language was English."

### Marin — FFY2025 *(Bank of Marin)*
- **Expedited service** (l.1207): "The county is not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing. On October 1, 2019, the county was placed on a Timeliness Corrective [Action Plan]…"
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.251): "…the CW 2200 form, denial NOA, and approval NOA were sent to the household in English when their preferred written language was Russian."
- **Over-verification / single type** (l.~253): "…it limited the income verification to 'paystubs.'"
- **Confidentiality** (l.44): "Confidentiality was not consistently maintained."

### Sonoma — FFY2024 *(Bank of Marin)*
- **Expedited service (longest-running CAP)** (l.1154): "The county is not meeting the performance goal of 90 percent for three-day ES processing. On September 17, 2018, a letter was sent to inform the county of the requirement to develop, submit, and implement a Timeliness Corrective Action Plan (CAP)."
- **30-day processing** (l.48): "Applications were not processed within the mandated 30-day time frame."
- **Confidentiality** (l.48): "Confidentiality was not maintained when computer stations were left unlocked."

### San Francisco — FFY2024 *(Bank of Marin)*
- **Wrong-language notice** (l.268): "…the approval NOA was sent to the household in English when their preferred language was Chinese."
- **Confidentiality** (l.47): "Confidentiality was not consistently maintained."
- **Voter registration** (l.47): "Voter Registration was not consistently offered." *(SF also documents cases decided without an interview — pull the exact recert/denial sentence from the report before quoting.)*
