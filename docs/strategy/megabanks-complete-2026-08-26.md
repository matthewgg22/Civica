# Wells' delineation, and the megabank sweep

**2026-08-26.** Fifteen OCC banks over $100B were fetched and read. Five are loaded.

## Wells is unblocked — the counties were read, not borrowed

Appendix A of the February 2023 evaluation lists the Los Angeles CSA as
**Anaheim-Santa Ana-Irvine MD (Orange), Los Angeles-Long Beach-Glendale MD (Los Angeles),
Oxnard-Thousand Oaks-Ventura MSA (Ventura), Riverside-San Bernardino-Ontario MSA (Riverside,
San Bernardino)** — the same five counties Chase and Bank of America use. The borrowed set
was right, but it is now *read* rather than assumed, which is the only version that may be
printed.

**Wells Fargo, Los Angeles CSA: 1,051 grants and donations totaling $80,000,000** — the
largest disclosed assessment-area giving figure anywhere in this project.

All five are **peers** — Outstanding or High Satisfactory on Service in the relevant rated
area, none with a gap to name.

TD and PNC were chosen deliberately over larger banks: **Philadelphia and Louisville each had
only two roster banks**, and these were the two megabanks whose disclosed giving landed in a
thin county rather than a crowded one.

## Three banks, three different Louisville areas

PNC's Louisville AA is **Bullitt, Jefferson, Oldham (KY) and Clark, Floyd, Washington (IN)**.
Stock Yards includes **Shelby** and excludes Washington. Republic differs from both again.

Three institutions, one metro, three county sets. This is why an assessment area is read from
each bank's own evaluation and never copied across.

## What the other ten do not disclose

US Bank, Capital One, Citi, Fifth Third, BMO, Citizens, HSBC, Huntington, KeyBank and
Santander were all fetched and searched. **None publishes a per-assessment-area
grants-and-donations figure** in a form this pass could extract — the same limited-scope
pattern found across the smaller banks, which now holds at every size.

The count stands at **seventeen banks read, nine with a usable figure**.

## The trap, again — and what it produced

A first pass on Wells matched *"106 grants and donations totaling $5.5 million"* just after a
Los Angeles mention. **That is the Fresno CSA.** Re-anchoring on the section that opens with
the assessment area's own name gave $80 million — and, incidentally, Wells' giving for
roughly **75 assessment areas**: Washington CSA $164.2M, New York CSA $95.7M, San Jose $88.5M,
Philadelphia $51M, Salt Lake City $55.5M, Atlanta $39.8M, Minneapolis $38M, Phoenix $19.9M,
Raleigh $17.2M, Dallas $14.5M, Portland $13.7M, San Diego $11M.

**That single table can seed depth in nearly every county on the board.**
