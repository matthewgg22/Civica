# Harris County: finding a funder

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Scan record:** `data-ops/analysis/bank-pe-mining/harris_occ_fed_2026.csv`

Harris is the **2nd largest SNAP enrollment gap in the country** and had just lost its most
promising candidate: Western Alliance listed a Houston assessment area in its 2024–2026
strategic plan, but the branch it was contingent on never opened, so Harris is not in a
Western Alliance assessment area.

The FDIC pass had already produced two Harris targets. What had never been run there was the
OCC/Fed route — the same gap that made Maricopa look empty. Running it produced **two new
targets**, both Fed-supervised and therefore absent from CRAPES entirely.

## The answer: Texas First Bank

| | |
|---|---|
| Regulator | Federal Reserve (RSSD 473266, FDIC cert 23799) |
| Assets | $2.4B |
| PE | May 28, 2024 |
| **Investment Test** | **Low Satisfactory** (Lending High Satisfactory; overall Satisfactory) |
| Houston AA | Brazoria, Chambers, Fort Bend, Galveston, **Harris**, Liberty, Montgomery |
| Donations in that AA | **36 totaling $258,000** — Community Services alone 32 / $156,000 |
| Ask | **$15,000** (~3× their average community-services donation in the AA) |

Two things make this the primary. The **Investment Test is the one a cash grant feeds** — a
grant is a qualified investment under §\_\_.12(t), not a community development service — and
it is their weak component. And **Houston is not a peripheral market for them**: 24
full-service branches, 0.6% deposit share, 19th of 101 institutions in the AA. A grant here
lands in the assessment area that decides their rating.

Note the bank **excludes Austin and Waller counties** from the Houston MSA in its own
delineation. We exclude them too. Their separate Beaumont AA (Hardin, Jefferson) is not part
of this pitch.

## The secondary: Third Coast Bank

| | |
|---|---|
| Regulator | Federal Reserve (RSSD 3630323, cert 58716) |
| Assets | $6.6B — headquartered in Humble, **Harris County** |
| PE | Aug 12, 2024 |
| **Investment Test** | **Low Satisfactory** |
| **Service Test** | **Low Satisfactory** |
| Houston AA | Brazoria, **Harris**, Montgomery — full-scope, the bank's heaviest-weighted AA |
| Donations in that AA | **30 totaling $65,000** — Community Services 19 / $34,000 |
| Ask | **$10,000** |

The only Harris-headquartered bank with **both** components our activity feeds rated Low
Satisfactory. In the Houston AA the PE says the bank is *"rarely in a leadership position"*
and makes *"rare use of innovative and/or complex investments."*

**Trap, recorded so nobody walks into it.** The most quotable sentence in this PE — *"the
bank only made four qualified donations totaling approximately $7,000"* — is about the
**Austin** assessment area, not Houston. Using it against Houston would be a false statement
to a bank about its own exam. Third Coast delineates **seven** AAs; they must not be merged.

## Harris now has four candidates

| Bank | Regulator | Assets | Gap | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Texas First Bank | FED | $2.4B | Investment Low Satisfactory | $12,500 |
| Prosperity Bank | FDIC | $38.3B | Service Low Satisfactory | $15,000 |
| Third Coast Bank | FED | $6.6B | Investment **and** Service Low Satisfactory | $7,500 (pool) |
| ~~Independent Bank~~ | FDIC | $18.3B | — | **DEAD** |

**Independent Bank closed on 2025-01-01** and was removed on 2026-08-25 by the liveness
check. Its PE ratings had been confirmed accurate against the evaluation earlier the same
day — which is precisely the trap: a *correct* rating on an institution that no longer
exists. Ratings verification and liveness verification are separate checks and neither
substitutes for the other.

Prosperity's ratings were also spot-checked against its PE and stand. Overlap on Harris is
total among the three survivors, so the pro rata attribution rule applies if more than one
funds.

## Recorded negatives

- **Woodforest National Bank** (OCC, $9.2B) has the **largest disclosed Houston giving in the
  entire dataset — $17.8M** — and is rated **Outstanding on all three tests**. No examination
  pressure at all. It is a capacity play for later, not a first pitch, and it is the clearest
  case in the roster that giving and pressure are different axes.
- **American First National** — Outstanding, Investment High Satisfactory.
- **Golden Bank, Central Bank** — small-bank/ISB evaluations with no Investment or Service
  component to be pressured.
- **Wells Fargo Bank South Central NA** — no evaluation at the probed OCC paths; a subsidiary
  charter whose CRA performance rolls up to the parent.
