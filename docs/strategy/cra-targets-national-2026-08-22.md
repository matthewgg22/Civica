# National CRA Target List — Banks Under CRA Pressure × County Need

Compiled 2026-08-22. Three verified layers crossed: (1) current "Needs to Improve"/"Substantial Noncompliance" ratings — computed from the full FFIEC interagency ratings database (83,931 exams, current through mid-2026; every bank listed has NO subsequent upgrade on record); (2) FDIC Q3+Q4 2026 + OCC Q3 2026 CRA exam schedules; (3) active DOJ redlining consent orders with unspent community-investment commitments. Need figures = eligible-unenrolled persons from our county fact bases (130% gross-income basis, upper-range; data-ops/sample/*-snap-gap/).

**Why pressure matters:** an NTI-rated bank must show new CRA-creditable activity before its follow-up exam (NTI triggers accelerated ~12-month re-exams); an exam-scheduled bank can book a grant into the evaluation period being examined; a consent-order bank has committed dollars that MUST be spent in named geographies. All three buy faster than a comfortable Satisfactory bank.

## Caveats & verification protocol

- Ratings publish 45–60+ days post-exam; corporate existence not re-verified — **BankFind status check before any outreach** (the generator's CERT-active discipline applies).
- Exam schedules: OCC Q4 2026 NOT yet published (supervisory-process reforms delayed it) — **re-check occ.gov late September**; AZ/SC emptiness is partly this gap.
- Need figures are our upper-range gross-income fact-base numbers — use with the standard bias disclosures; NTI-bank outreach should lead with "creditable, documented activity before your next exam," consent-order banks with the outreach/partnership line items their orders require.
- Every new target bank follows the loaded pattern: read its PE (AA counties) → add to assessment_areas.json → generate. Non-CA states need the generator's state-parameterization first (small task, not yet built).
