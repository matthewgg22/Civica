# Demeter outreach category list — re-prepared from the full CA ME audit

**Date:** 2026-09-11 · **Supersedes** the "Target the under-enrolled" list built from the partial audit (issue #1117). **Evidence base:** all 36 California CalFresh Management Evaluation reviews (CDSS FOIA R012681, FFY 2024–2025) + the five-family verdict mining. Each category names the target segment, the documented evidence, and the Demeter angle. No hardcoded dollar figures.

Priority key: **P1** = largest / most-documented lever · **P2** = strong, specific · **P3** = targeted / niche.

---

## A. LEP language communities — a multilingual bot is the *stated* remedy · **P1**
Wrong-language notices are a named finding in **16 of 38 counties**, across **8 preferred languages**. Most counties staff **Spanish only** and route the rest to an often-unreachable phone line — the exact capacity a chatbot substitutes for. Prioritized by documented failure + differentiation:

| Language | Counties with documented failures / gaps | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Spanish** | Monterey, SLO, San Bernardino, Santa Clara, Contra Costa, LA, Madera, Stanislaus, Yolo, Alameda, SF, San Benito, Sacramento | near-universal; base layer |

## B. Procedural-rights / wrongful-denial defense — eligible people denied on process · **P1**
Procedural/negative-action error (CAPER) runs 50–100% in many counties even at 0% payment error. Surface the rights that stop a wrongful cutoff:

- **You have the full 30 days** to submit verifications — the county shouldn't deny before then (SLO denied cases #3/#4/#6/#7). → corpus #1127
- **Interview before any denial**; a "no-show" should be followed up, not auto-denied (San Francisco). → #1127
- **Delayed processing / restoration of aid** after a missed recert — you often don't have to reapply (Shasta reception told households to reapply; Riverside FFY25). → #1127
- **Correct income limits by household size** so nobody self-deselects on a bad "you're over income" message (Yolo: household wrongly told over-income and withdrew). → #1127
- **A photo ID is enough** proof of identity; you can name an **authorized representative** (San Francisco). → #1127
- **What CalFresh can and cannot require** — over-verification is named in **34 of 38 counties** (sworn statements, bank balances, immunization, citizenship already in MEDS, "proof you don't receive income"). → corpus #1125

## C. Expedited-service (3-day) self-screen — the neediest wait longest · **P1**
Twelve-plus counties sit on 3-day-ES Timeliness Corrective Action Plans (Sonoma since 2018; Stanislaus since 2019; Kern/Yolo/San Bernardino since 2022; Ventura/Shasta since 2023; Merced 2024; Butte 2025). ES criteria are also frequently **not explained to callers**.
**Segment:** near-zero-income / destitute applicants, migrant/seasonal workers, households whose rent+utilities exceed income.
**Angle:** screen for ES entitlement up front; teach the 3-day right and postponed verification; what to do when the county misses it. → corpus #1126

---

## D. Broken-phone / off-hours alternative front door · **P2**
Counties where the call center auto-disconnects or holds 40 min–3+ hours: **Sacramento, San Bernardino, Fresno, Stanislaus, Madera, Humboldt, Tuolumne, Los Angeles, Santa Barbara, Ventura, Tulare, Butte.** Several under formal corrective action for callers who couldn't reach a human; some offices close by 3:30–4:00 pm.
**Angle:** position Demeter as the always-on channel — self-screen, understand rights, and start the application 24/7 without the jammed phone line. Media: emphasize evenings/weekends and mobile.

---

## E. ABAWD time-limit population (post-waiver) · **P2**
~**93.7%** of CA's time-limit caseload is now in non-waived counties — a concrete, datable population of ABAWDs 18–64 newly facing the 3-month clock (all San Joaquin Valley audit counties are ABAWD-waived, so target the non-waived footprint). Watch Alpine (waiver ending) and the OBBBA age-band change (18→64).
**Angle:** explain the current work rule, exemptions (incl. homelessness), and how to report qualifying hours (CF 887/888). → corpus #1113 (county-waiver data), #1116 (currency guard).

---

## F. SNAP-Ed channel collapse — step into the vacuum · **P2**
OBBB **§10107 defunded SNAP-Ed after FY2025**, shrinking the educator/food-bank outreach channels statewide exactly as Demeter can substitute. Counties with **no outstationed workers / no scheduled outreach** documented in the ME set (Shasta, Contra Costa, Tuolumne, Glenn, Sierra) are the clearest vacuums.
**Angle:** partner with the CBOs losing SNAP-Ed capacity; Demeter as the scalable education layer.

---

## G. Deduction-claim prompts — raise benefits, don't just enroll · **P3**
ME reviews repeatedly show **unclaimed deductions**: out-of-pocket **medical** for elderly/disabled not explored (Tulare, San Benito, Inyo), and **shelter/utilities** reported together instead of separately (a top CA payment-error driver).
**Segment:** 60+/disabled households; households with high rent+utilities.
**Angle:** prompt for the commonly-missed medical + SUA/shelter deductions; ESAP (Elderly Simplified Application) for 60+/disabled. → corpus `medical-deduction`, `sua-liheap-deduction` (live).

## What changed vs. the #1117 list
- Language category grew from 4 named gaps to **8 languages across 16 documented counties**, with per-county specificity and Mixtec confirmed as the differentiator.
- Procedural-rights category is now backed by **34/38 over-verification** and **50–100% CAPER** hard counts, and split into the specific rights (30-day, interview-first, no-reapply, income thresholds, ID/AR) that became corpus issues #1125/#1127.
- Added **Expedited-service (C)** and **Broken-phone (D)** as their own P1/P2 categories — the audit showed these are the dominant systemic barriers, not sub-points.
- Kept ABAWD (E) and SNAP-Ed (F); added the documented no-outreach counties.
