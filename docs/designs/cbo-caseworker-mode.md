## Problem Statement

CBO caseworkers do field enrollment in client homes, community events, and co-located clinics. The Civica iOS app today is built for the household to use alone. There is no surface that supports the field-assistance workflow: caseworker authenticates, helps a client through intake on-site, captures documents from what the client already has on their phone, walks them through eligibility live, and then transfers ownership of the packet to the client's own device so the household becomes a Civica user from that moment forward.

The CBO SaaS plan (2026-05-26) commits to selling "(1) applicant mobile UX, (2) dry-run packet accuracy, (3) compressed approval uncertainty" to CBO buyers at $1.8K–$12K/yr per org. Pillar 2 has a 2-3mo engine build on a separate critical path; pillar 3 is a marketing claim that requires pillar 1 to render. Pillar 1 is unspecified beyond "exists." Without a caseworker mode surface in iOS, the CBO buyer's experience of Civica is identical to GetCalFresh's — a web form a navigator types into. The unique-to-iOS capabilities (camera capture, session handoff to client device, live eligibility narration) are what differentiate the SaaS pitch.

## Demand Evidence

- **Direct observation** (user-reported in this session): caseworker re-keyed data the client already had on their own phone (paystub email, bank screenshots) instead of capturing direct. Caseworker also lost client follow-through after the visit — no way for client to add a doc later, no status visibility, no async question channel.
- **Project Bread field signal (MA):** In conversations with prospective SNAP enrollees, clients commonly say they'll finish the application "on their desktop later" — then don't. Clients have ongoing eligibility questions and operate over phone, not desktop. This is the load-bearing failure mode this design fixes: the wedge (session handoff onto the client's phone) is the structural answer to the "I'll do it on desktop" → never-completes gap, and the live eligibility narration is the answer to the phone-based question stream. Project Bread is a candidate for the named-pilot Assignment (see bottom of doc) — MA is in `supportedStateCodes` per the 2026-05-13 launch-state decision.
- **Active CBO SaaS plan dependency:** pillar 1 of the three-pillar pitch to mid-size multi-program CBOs requires this surface to exist. Without it, the pitch collapses to a dry-run engine sold to GetCalFresh users, which is a different product.
- **Audit trail demand:** USDA SNAP outreach grant renewal and CBO grant reporting require per-navigator attribution of every household-touch action. Current household-only iOS surface has no concept of attributed multi-actor edits.

## Status Quo

- Caseworkers type into BenefitsCal or GetCalFresh on a laptop. They photograph client documents with their personal phone, then email or AirDrop to their work device, then upload via web form. Re-keying happens because the data flow goes through the caseworker's eyes, not direct from client device to packet.
- Post-visit follow-up is text messages and phone calls. Document chase happens via email attachments or in-person second visits. Drop-off is high.
- Civica iOS today has no caseworker concept. No deep-link auth path. No role-gated UI. Packets are single-actor.
- Existing dashboard `/cbo-preview` is public and has a sample-packet queue but no concept of "field session in progress" or per-caseworker attribution.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**Target user:** CBO field caseworker doing assisted SNAP enrollment in client homes, community events, or co-located settings. CBO is mid-size multi-program (5-15 navigators per org per the CBO SaaS plan).

**Narrowest wedge (chosen in this session):** Session handoff — caseworker starts the packet, hands it off to the client's phone at end of visit. Client owns status tracking, document upload, and async question channel from that moment.

Wedge is load-bearing because it converts the caseworker visit from "navigator types data into a form" into "Civica becomes the household's app." Without handoff, every other caseworker feature still leaves Civica as a navigator tool; with handoff, every successful visit acquires a new daily-use Civica household.

## Constraints

- **One App Store binary.** No separate "Civica for CBOs" listing. Universal iOS app (phone + iPad). Role-gated routes inside the existing Civica iOS target.
- **Civica vs VoteNow target split.** This feature lives in the Civica target only. See `/target` skill and the two-target Xcode project memory.
- **Identity = Supabase role claim.** `cbo_caseworker` and `cbo_admin` roles. Deep-link auth opens the caseworker route on iOS. Web dashboard already has CBO role wiring per `project_uat_credentials` memory.
- **Strings parity required.** Every CivicaString gets `.en` and `.es` from day one. EBT pattern (`EBTStringParityTests`) is the template; mirror it for caseworker strings.
- **Audit attribution mandatory.** Every action by a caseworker in assisted mode writes `(caseworker_id, household_id, action_type, timestamp)` for grant reporting + the future grant-reportable PER export already in CBO SaaS scope.
- **Branch hygiene.** Per `feedback_branch_hygiene` memory — config/Info.plist/xcconfig edits do NOT bundle with feature work. Land any infra setup on `claude/civica-snap-v1` first.
- **YC submission timing.** CBO SaaS plan implies the demo story matters. Approach B is sized at ~4-6 weeks human / ~3-5 days CC — fits a YC narrative without rushing.

## Premises (confirmed in this session)

1. ~~iOS phone only~~ → **REVISED:** All three surfaces — iOS phone, iPad (universal app), and web dashboard extension. Same caseworker identity across all three.
2. **Caseworker uses their own attributed Civica account.** Not a borrowed login, not impersonation. Every action audit-logged with caseworker identity + household identity.
3. **Handoff creates (or claims) the household account on the client's device.** This is how Civica acquires the household as a daily-use user via the caseworker visit. Load-bearing for lifecycle ownership.
4. **One packet, multiple actors.** Caseworker and client edit the same record via row-level role permissions. No fork-and-merge data model.
5. ~~Semi-hidden tab via org code~~ → **REVISED:** Access via separate sign-in deep link (e.g., `civica://caseworker` or magic-link from CBO invite email). Same binary, hidden entry — not org code, not separate App Store listing.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A — Magic Handoff Wedge (minimal viable)
Summary: Ship only the session-handoff capability on iOS + minimal caseworker queue view on web. Defer doc capture, live eligibility, iPad, full role-gated UI tree.
Effort: S-M (~1.5-2 weeks human / ~1-2 days CC)
Risk: Low
Reuses: Supabase auth + magic link, existing packet model, `apps/dashboard/lib/packet-fetchers.ts`, `apps/dashboard/app/cbo-preview/`
Pros:
- Fastest path to a unique-to-iOS demo moment.
- Clean proof that Civica becomes the household's app post-visit.
Cons:
- One feature alone won't carry a CBO sale at $1.8K-$12K/yr.
- Re-keying pain (the other observed pain point) remains unaddressed.
- Audit trail / grant-reportable PER export still blocked.

### Approach B — Caseworker Mode Surface (ideal architecture) — SELECTED
Summary: Full role-gated iOS UI tree (universal — phone + iPad). Queue → packet detail with shared edit → camera doc capture → live eligibility narration (snap-rules engine, "explain to client" mode) → session handoff. Web dashboard caseworker view extends `/cbo-preview` with field-session liveness. Audit logging attributes every action.
Effort: M-L (~4-6 weeks human / ~3-5 days CC)
Risk: Medium
Reuses: snap-rules engine (already shipped per `project_snap_rules_engine_ios`), EBT capture patterns (camera + parser, per EBTBalance scaffold), `/cbo-preview` route, dashboard packet fetchers, Supabase RLS scaffolding, EBT strings-parity test template.
Pros:
- Delivers all three CBO SaaS pillars on iOS in one coherent surface.
- Audit trail makes the grant-reportable PER export real (already in CBO SaaS accepted scope).
- iPad ships for free with universal app.
- Identity story is single-coherent across phone, iPad, web.
- Dry-run engine plugs in as a render target when its 2-3mo build lands — no rework.
Cons:
- More surface area to QA.
- 4-6 weeks human / 3-5 days CC outside the absolute fastest possible YC sprint.

### Approach C — Two-App Split (contrarian)
Summary: Separate "Civica for Caseworkers" iOS app. Households use existing Civica. Handoff = deep link from caseworker app opens consumer Civica.
Effort: M (~3-4 wks) plus doubled ongoing maintenance
Risk: Medium-High
Reuses: Most of consumer Civica, but doubles release/QA/review pipeline
Pros:
- Clean code separation; caseworker iteration without consumer-app regression risk.
- "Civica for CBOs" App Store positioning as marketing.
Cons:
- User explicitly rejected separate App Store listing in P5.
- Two binaries, two release pipelines, two review queues.
- Fragments identity story; weakens "one platform" pitch to CBOs and YC.
- Higher total maintenance forever, not just at v1.

## Recommended Approach: Approach B

The CBO SaaS plan (2026-05-26) sells three pillars together; Approach A delivers one. Approach C is rejected by P5. Approach B's iPad+web costs are marginal (universal app + existing `/cbo-preview`), and its audit-trail foundation unblocks the grant-reportable PER export already in CBO SaaS accepted scope. The dry-run engine lives on a separate critical path; Approach B ships now and plugs the engine in as a render target when it lands.

## Core Features (Approach B scope)

1. **Deep-link caseworker sign-in.** `civica://caseworker?invite={token}` or `https://civica.app/cbo/sign-in?invite={token}` opens iOS Civica into caseworker route. Magic-link auth via Supabase. `cbo_caseworker` or `cbo_admin` role claim required.
2. **Caseworker queue.** List of households assigned to this caseworker, with status badges (in progress, awaiting docs, submitted, approved, recert due). Filterable by status, sortable by last activity. Pulls from the same Supabase packets table as the household view, scoped by RLS.
3. **Packet detail with shared edit.** Caseworker opens a packet, sees the same screens the household sees, but with caseworker affordances: who-edited-what column, attribution timestamps, "add a note for this household" field. RLS allows the caseworker to edit on behalf of the household; every edit writes the attribution row.
4. **Camera doc capture in field mode.** Camera button on every doc-required field. Captures, OCRs (server-side or on-device — TBD in eng review), prefills the field, lets caseworker confirm. Photo stored encrypted, attached to the packet. Reuses EBT capture patterns where possible.
5. **Live eligibility narration.** When the dry-run / snap-rules engine has output, caseworker can flip into "explain to client" mode — big text, plain language, line-by-line walk-through of why this packet should pass or what's missing. Without the dry-run engine yet, this uses the existing `snap-rules` evaluateChecklist output.
6. **Session handoff (the wedge).** "Hand off to client" button at end of visit. Generates a short-lived signed JWT in a QR code + tappable magic link. Client scans (or taps if AirDropped/messaged), Civica opens on client's device, packet now visible in client's household view. Client identity provisioned (or linked to existing household account) at handoff time. Caseworker retains read access + status visibility.
7. **Field-session liveness on web dashboard.** Extend `/cbo-preview` (or a new `/cbo` authenticated route) so a CBO admin can see which caseworkers have active field sessions, which packets are in flight, what docs just arrived. Surfaces the doc-arrival event from #4 as a notification.
8. **Audit log.** Every caseworker action writes `(caseworker_id, household_id, action_type, payload_summary, timestamp)`. Feeds the grant-reportable PER export already in CBO SaaS scope.
9. **Strings + i18n.** Every new CivicaString has `.en` and `.es` from day one. New `Strings/CBO{Concern}Strings.swift` files following the EBT split pattern. `CBOStringParityTests` mirrors `EBTStringParityTests`.

## Open Questions

- **OCR engine choice.** On-device (VisionKit / Apple TextRecognition) vs server-side (existing scraper infra + Tesseract or a vendor)? On-device is faster + private; server-side benefits from improvement over time. Defer to `/plan-eng-review`.
- **Handoff identity model.** Does the client's scan create a NEW household account, or claim an existing one (e.g., when the client already has a Civica account from a prior outreach touchpoint)? Probably both, with idempotent claim logic — eng review territory.
- **Offline tolerance.** Rural CA has bad signal. Minimum: capture docs offline and sync later. Maximum: full offline-tolerant packet edit with conflict resolution. Probably the minimum for v1, the maximum deferred to TODOS.
- **CBO admin provisioning.** Who creates `cbo_caseworker` accounts? Self-serve from a CBO admin via dashboard, vs Civica-team-manual-provision via Supabase. Self-serve is the right answer; specify the dashboard flow in eng review.
- **Pricing implication.** Does caseworker count drive the SaaS price tier? Per CBO SaaS plan it's $1.8K-$12K/yr based on "navigator count + enrollment volume" — confirm caseworker = navigator and lock the per-seat math.
- **Recert lifecycle handoff.** When a packet hits recert (12 months later), does the caseworker get notified, the household, or both? CBO SaaS plan claims lifecycle ownership through recert + appeals — specify the handback-to-caseworker flow.

## Success Criteria

- Caseworker can sign in via deep link, see their queue, open a packet, capture a document via camera, and hand off the packet to a client's device in under 5 minutes — observed in real field conditions, not a demo room.
- Every action attributed in the audit log; PER export shows per-caseworker breakdown.
- One CBO partner running 5+ caseworkers through Civica end-to-end on a paid plan within 90 days of v1 ship.
- iPad universal-app behavior verified on real iPad in the field, not just simulator.
- Strings parity test passes; no Spanish gaps.

## Eng Review Decisions (locked 2026-05-27)

- **A1A — Caseworker identity = `caseworker_assignments` table.** NOT a global `app_metadata.role` claim. Same auth user can be household + caseworker + multi-CBO simultaneously. Avoids the buddy-role gotcha by design.
- **A2A — Handoff security = one-time ticket + phone-OTP bind.** Server-side `handoff_tickets` row, 15-min expiry, single-use enforcement via atomic `consumed_at` set, client identity bound to phone number caseworker enters at QR generation. Defeats coffee-shop-screenshot attack.
- **A3A — Concurrent edit = optimistic version column + 409.** `packets.version` int incremented on write; mismatched base version → 409, client refresh-and-retry, UI surfaces "{Caseworker} updated this — refresh."
- **A4 — Civica target only.** Caseworker `Info.plist` URL scheme + AASA entitlements on Civica target only. Use `/target` to verify.
- **A5 — Offline doc queue.** Encrypted local cache via Keychain pattern. Each queued doc stamped with `caseworker_id` at capture time, NEVER re-attributed to household post-handoff.
- **A6 — Deep link = Universal Links primary** (`https://civica.app/cbo/sign-in/*`). AASA from `civica-api` Vercel project. Custom `civica://` as dev fallback.
- **C1A — View sharing = parametrized `viewerRole: PacketViewerRole` enum.** Same SwiftUI views for household + caseworker; affordances are conditional modifiers, not separate screens.
- **C2 — Audit log centralized at gateway middleware** (`withCaseworkerAudit`). Single source of truth; can't be bypassed by adding a new endpoint.
- **C3 — Named exceptions for handoff failures**: `HandoffTicketExpired`, `HandoffTicketAlreadyConsumed`, `HandoffPhoneOTPMismatch`, `HandoffCaseworkerSessionEnded`. No bare `catch (Exception)`.
- **TR1B — New `/v1/cbo/handoff` endpoint** (NOT consolidating with existing `/v1/enrollment/.../handoff`). Clean separation between dashboard-navigator and CBO-caseworker flows. T17 captures the post-v1 consolidation audit.
- **T1A — Full ★★★ coverage on 29 paths**, including critical regression test for household-mode packet view unchanged after `viewerRole` parametrization.
- **P1 — Queue queries via single joined fetcher** (`apps/dashboard/lib/packet-fetchers.ts`).
- **P2 — Handoff ticket cleanup** extends existing 5-min buddy-cleanup cron.
- **P3 — Doc OCR runs in background**, never blocks UI.
- **P4 — Field-session liveness via Supabase Realtime**, polling fallback.
- **P5 — Rate limits**: 60/min standard on cbo namespace; 10/min strict on handoff mint.
