Under § 552(a)(6)(C), a requester may treat an agency's failure to meet the statutory deadline as a **constructive denial** and either (a) file an administrative appeal to force a determination, or (b) proceed directly to district court, without needing to exhaust further agency-level waiting. ## Compliance defensive-language pattern (locked in across all filings)

Every CPRA/PIA/FOIA filing includes:

1. **§ 10850 / individual-records disclaim** (state filings) — pre-empts the auto-deflection template
2. **HKS educational institution affiliation** — invokes federal educational fee category (5 USC § 552(a)(4)(A)(ii)(II)) where applicable
3. **Non-commercial scholarly use** — anchors public-interest fee waiver
4. **"Final / issued / distributed / transmitted"** framing — strips § 7927.500 deliberative-process exemption attack surface
5. **"Existing electronic records only"** — preempts "we'd have to create new records" defense (CPRA *Sander* rule and federal analog)
6. **"In whatever form maintained"** — accepts existing format, doesn't demand reformatting
7. **Segregability clause** — forces partial production rather than blanket denial
8. **Small-cell suppression offered** (data requests) — defuses re-identification objections
9. **§ 7922.570 / equivalent electronic-format invocation** — forces electronic production
10. **Vendor-IP / security-infrastructure carve-outs** (CalSAWS) — pre-scopes around § 7929.210
11. **HMIS confidentiality acknowledged** (Homeless Services) — scopes around 24 CFR § 580.31
12. **(b)(7) law-enforcement redaction acceptance** (OIG only) — gets past investigative-records reflex
13. **"Closed" / "program-level not individual"** (OIG only) — converts investigative records into producible scope
14. **Division routing recommendations** — speeds intra-agency forwarding
15. **§ 7922.000 exemption-particularity demand** (new, 2026-08-15) — added to the LA DPSS Request 6 follow-up: an agency must identify the specific statutory exemption for anything withheld, not just recite generic non-waiver boilerplate. Worth folding into future follow-up letters generally, not just this one.

## Notes on selection logic (for paper methods section)

**Three-county California sample logic:**
- **LA County (urban, non-waiver, ~25% of state caseload)** — statistical dominance anchor
- **Fresno County (interior agricultural, non-waiver, ~4% of state caseload)** — Central Valley natural-experiment control
- **Tulare County (interior agricultural, waiver-designated, ~2% of state caseload)** — Central Valley waiver counterfactual to Fresno

This gives volume coverage (~31% of state caseload), waiver natural experiment (Tulare vs Fresno with comparable economic profiles), and geographic variation (LA Basin + Central Valley).

**Texas comparator logic:**
- Maximum variation on every dimension that matters for OBBBA implementation: admin architecture (state vs county), error-rate baseline (8.32% vs ~7%), waiver pursuit (none vs 7 counties), pending federal sanctions ($709M vs none), policy posture (eligibility enforcement vs caseload protection).
