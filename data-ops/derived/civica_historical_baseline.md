## 1 · OBBBA Liability Framing

P.L. 119-21 (OBBBA) §10105 takes effect FY2028, converting payment-error rate (PER) into a federally-mandated state cost-share liability:

| PER band | State share | Federal share |
|---|---|---|
| < 6% | 0% | 100% |
| 6% to < 8% | 5% | 95% |
| 8% to < 10% | 10% | 90% |
| ≥ 10% | 15% | 85% |

Delayed-implementation clause §10105(B)(iii): states whose FY25 PER × 1.5 ≥ 20% (i.e. ≥ 13.33%) are pushed to FY2029. This is the cliff documented in `~/Desktop/Civica USDA data/deliverables/civica_10105_fy29_cliff.pdf`.

**The pitch:** every percentage point of PER above 6% maps to real state-budget dollars starting FY28. The federal QC ELEMENT codes below enumerate what drives error. Each category is a wedge where a Civica control either already exists or names the gap.
