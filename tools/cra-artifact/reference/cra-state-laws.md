# CRA — State "Mini-CRA" Laws Reference

Companion to [`cra-framework.md`](./cra-framework.md), which covers the **federal**
CRA and explicitly scoped out state laws. This file covers the **state-level**
Community Reinvestment Acts ("mini-CRAs") — which states have one, whom each
covers, and what it means for the bank-pitch channel. Like the federal file, it
is a corpus, **not legal advice**, and does not decide how any activity is
treated — that rests with the relevant regulator and examiners.

- **Compiled:** 2026-09-12
- **Authoritative frame:** the CFPB's **"State Community Reinvestment Acts:
  Summary of State Laws" (Nov 2023)**, which reviewed all 50 states, DC, and the
  territories and identified an affirmative-obligation state CRA in exactly
  **seven states plus DC**. Each is cross-checked here against the state statute.
  <https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_state_community_reinvestment_acts_2023-11.pdf>
- **Sourcing note:** IL, WA, and DC were verified to statute text; MA/NY/CT/RI/WV
  existence, coverage, and regulator were confirmed via the CFPB survey and state
  regulator pages, but a few section numbers and two enactment years remain to be
  verified verbatim (see [§6 Confidence & caveats](#6-confidence--caveats)).

---

## 0. Bottom line for the pitch

- **The enacted universe is eight jurisdictions:** **MA, NY, CT, RI, WV, IL, WA,
  and DC.** No other state has an operative state CRA as of 2026-09-12.
- **None of Civica's current target markets or corpus states has an operative
  state CRA.** California and Florida — where all ten target banks sit — rely on
  the **federal** CRA only. So a state-CRA angle **adds no new hook to the
  current CA/FL pitch.**
- **One watch item:** **California AB 801** passed the legislature and was
  presented to the Governor on **Sept 3, 2026** — awaiting signature, **not yet
  law**. If signed, it would add DFPI exams of state banks, credit unions, and
  mortgage lenders. Re-check its status before relying on it either way.
- **Where state CRA *does* become a lever is expansion.** Four of Civica's
  corpus states — **NY, MA, IL, WA** — have state CRAs, and NY/MA/IL extend
  beyond banks to **credit unions and/or nonbank mortgage lenders**. That widens
  the potential funder universe in those states beyond the FDIC/OCC/FRB-examined
  banks the current channel targets.

Two structural patterns worth carrying into the pitch:
- **Modern, broad laws (IL 2021; NY 2021 expansion; MA 2007 expansion)** reach
  beyond banks to credit unions and/or nonbank mortgage lenders and run their own
  periodic exams — these create the most additional CRA-style demand.
  **The mortgage-lender extension is the driver of these modern laws:**
  independent/nonbank mortgage companies now originate the majority of
  home-purchase loans yet are **outside the federal CRA** (which reaches only
  insured depositories), so MA (2007), NY (2021), and IL (2021) each extended
  their state CRA to cover them. That is why "mortgage" looms large in the state
  regimes — it is the coverage gap they were written to close. (Practical effect
  for outreach: in those states the pool of CRA-obligated institutions includes
  large nonbank mortgage lenders, not just banks.)
- **Older, federal-reliant laws (RI, WV, WA, and CT to a degree)** lean on the
  federal rating and federally-set assessment areas, and several act only when an
  institution files an expansionary application — lighter touch.

## 2. Per-jurisdiction detail

### Massachusetts
- **Statute:** M.G.L. **ch. 167 §14** (banks; regs 209 CMR 46.00); **ch. 255E §8**
  (mortgage lenders; regs 209 CMR 54.00). §14 imposes a "continuing and
  affirmative obligation to help meet the credit needs" of local communities.
- **Enacted:** bank CRA commonly cited as **1982** (unverified this pass);
  amended by Ch. 238 of the Acts of **1996**; **mortgage-lender CRA added 2007**
  (Ch. 206), making MA an early state to apply CRA to nonbank mortgage lenders.
- **Regulator:** Massachusetts **Division of Banks**.
- **Covers:** state-chartered **banks**, **state credit unions** (via 209 CMR
  46), and **licensed mortgage lenders** making 50+ residential loans/year.
- **Rating:** own **five-tier** scale — Outstanding, High Satisfactory,
  Satisfactory, Needs to Improve, Substantial Noncompliance. Evaluations are
  **public** on mass.gov.
- **Federal interaction:** standalone state exams on state-designated assessment
  areas, often run concurrently with the federal exam; state issues its own PE.
- Statute: <https://malegislature.gov/Laws/GeneralLaws/PartI/TitleXXII/Chapter167/Section14>
  · Ratings: <https://www.mass.gov/info-details/cra-ratings-and-public-evaluations-for-banks-and-credit-unions>
  · Mortgage-lender CRA: <https://www.mass.gov/info-details/mortgage-lender-community-investment-cra-for-mortgage-lenders>

### New York
- **Statute:** **Banking Law §28-b** (NY CRA).
- **Enacted:** **1978** (among the earliest state CRAs). **2021** amendment
  (signed Nov 1, 2021) extended it to **nonbank mortgage lenders**; DFS
  implementing regs **effective Jan 7, 2026**, compliance date **July 7, 2026**.
- **Regulator:** NY **Department of Financial Services (DFS)**.
- **Covers:** state-chartered banking institutions (banks, trust companies,
  savings banks, S&Ls); since 2021, **licensed mortgage bankers**. (No evidence
  it covers credit unions.)
- **Rating:** **four-tier overall**, with **five-tier component** ratings for
  large banks. PEs **public** at DFS's CRA Exam Reports page (mortgage-company
  evaluation disclosure rules were still pending as of the CFPB report).
- **Federal interaction:** independent state exam on state-designated assessment
  areas; §28-b requires covered institutions to file their federal CRA
  reports with the superintendent; NY collects more small-business data than the
  federal CRA.
- Statute: <https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/BNK/28-B>
  · DFS about-CRA: <https://www.dfs.ny.gov/apps_and_licensing/banks_and_trusts/about_cra>
  · DFS exam reports: <https://www.dfs.ny.gov/reports_and_publications/exam_reports/cra_exam_reports>

### Connecticut
- **Statute:** **Conn. Gen. Stat. §§36a-30 to 36a-33** (formerly §§36-52a–d).
- **Enacted:** **1989** (P.A. 89-295), effective **July 1, 1990**.
- **Regulator:** Connecticut **Department of Banking**.
- **Covers:** state-chartered banks and **community credit unions** (only where
  the credit union has a **geographic** field of membership).
- **Rating:** **four-tier**. Periodic written PEs; the public section must go in
  the bank's public file within 30 business days of receipt. Public.
- **Federal interaction:** §36a-32 directs assessment "utilizing the applicable
  methodology set forth in federal CRA" — parallels the federal framework while
  adding state-specific factors.
- Statute: <https://law.justia.com/codes/connecticut/title-36a/chapter-664a/section-36a-30-formerly-sec-36-52a/>
  · History (PA 89-295): <https://www.cga.ct.gov/2003/rpt/2003-R-0157.htm>

### Rhode Island
- **Statute:** **R.I. Gen. Laws §19-9-4** ("Credit needs of local communities").
- **Enacted:** ~1980s (year unverified this pass).
- **Regulator:** RI **Department of Business Regulation — Division of Banking**.
- **Covers:** "regulated institutions" subject to the federal CRA, including
  **credit unions with a geographic field of membership** (mirrors CT).
- **Rating:** **no periodic state rating**; RI runs an **application-triggered**
  review (merger/branch/expansion) that considers the institution's **federal**
  CRA rating and factors like geographic credit distribution.
- **Federal interaction:** relies on federal assessment areas and the federal
  rating.
- Statute: <https://codes.findlaw.com/ri/title-19-financial-institutions/ri-gen-laws-sect-19-9-4/>

### West Virginia
- **Statute:** **W. Va. Code §§31A-8B-1 to 31A-8B-5** (Article 8B, "Community
  Reinvestment Act").
- **Enacted:** **1982** (SB 331).
- **Regulator:** WV **Division of Financial Institutions** (Commissioner of
  Banking; Board of Banking and Financial Institutions).
- **Covers:** WV-chartered **banks** (and holding companies). **Not** credit
  unions or nonbank mortgage lenders.
- **Rating:** **no state rating**; considers the **federal** rating in
  application reviews (deposit facility, acquisitions). Application-triggered.
- **Federal interaction:** strongly federal-dependent; uses federal assessment
  areas and the federal rating. The public record is the institution's **federal**
  PE.
- Statute: <https://code.wvlegislature.gov/31A-8B/>

### Illinois — the modern, broad law
- **Statute:** **Illinois Community Reinvestment Act, 205 ILCS 735** (Article 35
  of P.A. 101-0657).
- **Enacted:** **2021** (effective March 23, 2021); implementing rules **effective
  May 1, 2024** — so the operational exam regime is 2024-forward.
- **Regulator:** **IDFPR** — Division of Banking (state banks + covered nonbank
  mortgage lenders) and Division of Financial Institutions (state credit unions).
- **Covers:** state-chartered **banks**, state **credit unions**, and **nonbank
  residential mortgage licensees** — the first state to include mortgage
  companies in the original enactment.
- **Rating:** its own multi-tier system — a 4-point scale for mortgage companies,
  5-point component tests for banks/credit unions, and a 4-point overall scale;
  results made public via IDFPR.
- **Federal interaction:** complementary; reaches institutions (credit unions,
  nonbank mortgage lenders) the federal CRA does not, with its own
  lending/deposit-based assessment-area process.
- Statute: <https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ChapterID=20&ActID=4086>
  · IDFPR ILCRA: <https://idfpr.illinois.gov/il-cra.html>

### Washington — a light-touch 1985 law (NOT a 2023 rule)
> **Correction of a common error:** there is **no broad "2023 Washington state
> CRA."** Two 2023-era items are easy to mis-cite and are **not** a bank CRA: **SB
> 6025 (2024)** is a predatory-lending/Consumer Loan Act bill, and the WA
> Commerce **"Community Reinvestment Program"** is a cannabis social-equity grant
> program. Washington's actual bank CRA dates to 1985.
- **Statute:** **RCW ch. 30A.60** ("Community Credit Needs"; commercial banks) and
  **RCW ch. 32.40** (savings banks); rules at WAC ch. 208-512.
- **Enacted:** **1985** (1985 c 329), effective Jan 1, 1986; last substantively
  amended 2009.
- **Regulator:** Washington **Department of Financial Institutions (DFI),
  Division of Banks**.
- **Covers:** state-chartered commercial banks, savings banks, and savings
  associations. **Not credit unions** (Ch. 31.12 RCW) and **not** mortgage
  licensees.
- **Rating:** a **five-point numerical scale** — RCW 30A.60.010(3): 1 = excellent
  … 5 = poor.
- **Federal interaction:** no standalone periodic exam; the review runs alongside
  other state supervisory exams and **may rely on the federal PE**; uses
  federally-set assessment areas; enforced through application review.
- Statute: <https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=30A.60&full=true>
  · DFI Banks: <https://dfi.wa.gov/banks>

### District of Columbia
- **Statute:** **D.C. Code §26-431.01 to §26-431.08** ("Community Development Act
  of 2000," D.C. Law 13-308, effective June 9, 2001).
- **Regulator:** DC **Department of Insurance, Securities and Banking (DISB)**
  Commissioner.
- **Covers:** "deposit-receiving institutions" (banks, savings institutions,
  **credit unions**) that hold **District government contracts** — a
  linked-deposit-style trigger.
- **Mechanism:** requires a **community-development plan** with compliance
  monitoring and a **five-point** rating; the assessment area is the **entire
  District**; reviewed in cooperation with other regulators.
- Statute: <https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/titles/26/chapters/4A>

## 4. Common false positives (NOT state CRAs)

The CFPB survey deliberately excluded these, and so should any bank
communication:

- **Maryland** — community-reinvestment *information-sharing* between regulators
  only; no affirmative obligation.
- **Louisiana** — requires only *disclosure* of the bank's **federal** CRA rating.
- **Mississippi** — requires only *annual publication* of the **federal** CRA PE.
- **U.S. Virgin Islands** — requires *publication of CRA statements*, not a full
  exam regime.
- **Nevada NRS 665.180** — a federal-rating disclosure statute (see [§3](#3-target-market--corpus-states-status)).
- **Washington SB 6025** (predatory lending) and the WA Commerce **"Community
  Reinvestment Program"** (cannabis grants) — neither is a bank CRA.

Not-yet-law (do not cite as enacted): **CA AB 801** (awaiting signature);
**NY DFS** proposed CRA regs (Feb 5, 2025 rulemaking, not new statute);
**CT SB 1398** (bill to add independent mortgage bankers).

## 5. What this means for Civica

- **Current pitch (CA + FL): federal CRA is the only lever.** Neither state has an
  operative state CRA, so nothing in this file changes the current per-bank
  artifact. Do **not** invoke a "California CRA" — AB 801 is not law (yet).
- **Expansion states change the calculus.** In **NY, MA, and IL**, the state CRA
  reaches **credit unions and nonbank mortgage lenders** in addition to banks —
  so a benefits-access program there could be pitched to a broader set of
  regulated funders, several of which face **periodic state exams** (stronger,
  recurring demand than an application-triggered review).
- **The activity's eligibility travels.** State CRAs largely parallel the federal
  community-development framework, so "community services targeted to LMI
  individuals" ([`cra-framework.md` §8.1](./cra-framework.md#81-the-snap--lmi-proxy--__12g21-load-bearing-for-the-pitch))
  is the same CD hook under the state regimes — but each state's regulator and
  examiner make the determination, and the rating scales differ (e.g., MA's
  five-tier, WA/DC's five-point). Never assert state-CRA qualification; frame it
  the same way as the federal posture.
- **Watch AB 801.** If California enacts it, CA becomes the first target-market
  state with a state CRA — a material change worth a dedicated pass at that time.

## 6. Confidence & caveats

- **High confidence:** the eight-jurisdiction universe (MA, NY, CT, RI, WV, IL,
  WA, DC); that none of the ten target/corpus states has an operative state CRA;
  IL/WA/DC statute citations (verified to statute text); the WA correction (1985
  law, not a 2023 rule); the false-positive exclusions.
- **Verify before quoting verbatim to a bank:** the exact section numbers for
  **MA/NY/CT/RI/WV** (existence/coverage confirmed via the CFPB survey and
  regulator pages; section numbers not each pulled from raw statute this pass),
  and the two unverified enactment years — **MA bank CRA (~1982)** and **RI
  (~1980s)**.
- **Time-sensitive:** **CA AB 801** signature/veto status (presented to the
  Governor Sept 3, 2026) — re-check before any dated use; **NY** mortgage-lender
  CRA regs (compliance July 7, 2026) and **IL** rules (effective May 1, 2024) are
  recent and still bedding in.
- **Not legal advice.** State-CRA coverage and any qualification determination
  rest with the state regulator and examiners.
