What the pilot does

Factual, geo-targeted outreach ("You may qualify for [[program_ref]]. Check in 5 minutes.") across your assessment area drives residents to a free eligibility check and application support. We accept no per-enrollment compensation. A Google Ad Grant (up to $10,000/month of donated search advertising) stretches every grant dollar, so your funding primarily buys reach beyond the grant and the measurement itself.

What you receive

A quarterly report (sample in the appendix) documenting outreach delivered, residents served, and applications generated in your assessment area, with dollar-traceability from grant to outcome, prepared so your team can use it directly in CRA documentation. Activities of this type are typically considered community development services under the community-services prong; your compliance team makes that determination.

How measurement works

  • Layer 1 · observed: ad delivery reconciled to invoices; sessions, checks, applications started and submitted are recorded system events. This layer alone carries the CRA documentation.
  • Layer 2 · consented panel: opt-in 30/60-day check-in; opt-in and response rates disclosed, outcomes reported among respondents only, never extrapolated silently.
  • Layer 3 · corroboration: the state's published county caseload figures shown as directional context, not attribution; geographic holdout testing at multi-county scale.
Why we report this way. Payment is never tied to enrollment counts, ads are factual not persuasive, and projections are labeled until they are measurements. A report your examiner can trust is worth more than a bigger number.

Methodology & disclosures

  • [[method_bullet]]
  • Known bias, disclosed: the model uses a gross-income proxy for eligibility, and ACS respondents under-report benefit receipt. Both push the estimated eligible-unenrolled population upward. Read the figures as an upper-range estimate of unmet need.
  • County estimates carry 1-Year ACS sampling uncertainty; the page-1 dollar range reflects household-size assumptions ([[hh_high]]–[[hh_low]] persons/household), not a statistical confidence interval.
