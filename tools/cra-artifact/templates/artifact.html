What SNAP is

SNAP is the federal food-benefit program — monthly grocery money for low- and moderate-income households, spent at neighborhood stores. Most people who qualify but never enroll are stopped by the process, not the rules. Civica's free assistant, a chatbot grounded in your state's own SNAP rules, answers their questions in plain language and guides them through the application.

Measurement

  • The engine: a conversational assistant grounded in your state's SNAP rules answers questions, cites the governing rule, and routes to an application; everything below is measured from it.
  • Layer 1 · observed: ad delivery reconciled to invoices; sessions, checks, applications started and submitted are recorded system events. This layer alone carries the CRA documentation.
  • Layer 2 · consented panel: opt-in 30/60-day check-in; response rates disclosed, outcomes reported among respondents only, never extrapolated.
  • Layer 3 · corroboration: the state's published county caseload as directional context, not attribution; holdout testing at multi-county scale.
Reporting. Payment is never tied to enrollment counts, and projections stay labeled until they are measured. You receive an auditable record of activity in your assessment area.

Methodology & disclosures

  • Known bias, disclosed: a gross-income proxy and ACS under-reporting push the estimate upward — read it as an upper range of unmet need.
