What we measured, and what missed

The pilot's stated learning goal, now measured

No published benchmark covers the step from starting an eligibility check to submitting an application. Measuring it was this pilot's stated purpose. Result: session to completed check ran [[s2c_measured]] against the [[s2c_proposed]] range presented at proposal, and completed check to application started ran [[c2s_measured]] against [[c2s_proposed]]. Both landed at the conservative end. Applications submitted therefore came in at [[submitted]] against a [[proj_low_sub]]–[[proj_mid_sub]] projection.

What we are changing next quarter

  • Shorten the check itself — completion, not traffic, is the binding constraint.
  • Shift budget toward the counties with the strongest completed-check rates rather than the cheapest clicks.
  • Add a Spanish-language variant of the highest-drop-off step and report the language split separately next quarter.

Methodology and known limits

  • Delivery figures reconcile to platform invoices monthly. Service figures are recorded system events, not survey estimates.
  • Approval figures are estimates built from a consented panel; opt-in and response rates are stated on page 3 and never grossed up.
  • Need estimates are [[model_note]]. Known biases: the gross-income screen ignores asset tests and ACS respondents under-report benefit receipt — both push the eligible-unenrolled estimate upward. Read as an upper-range estimate of unmet need.
  • ZIP codes straddle county lines; ambiguous locations are excluded from county totals rather than assigned.
  • Compensation under this grant is not tied to applications or enrollments produced, and program materials never direct participants toward any institution's products.
