Primary purpose

The funded activity is SNAP outreach and application assistance directed to residents who screen as income-eligible. A majority of both the dollars and the beneficiaries are identifiable to the community development purpose above, satisfying the primary-purpose standard of the Interagency Questions and Answers Regarding Community Reinvestment (§__.12(h)—8). Funds support program delivery only.

Evidence of low- and moderate-income benefit

Participants receive, or screen as eligible to receive, SNAP — expressly listed in the Interagency Q&A as an accepted method of establishing LMI benefit (§__.12(g)(2)—1):

"…recipients of government assistance programs that have income qualifications equivalent to, or stricter than, the definitions of low- and moderate-income as defined by the CRA Regulations. Examples include … U.S. Department of Agriculture's … Supplemental Nutrition Assistance programs."

SNAP's gross-income limit (130% of the federal poverty guideline) is stricter than the CRA definition of moderate income in the geographies served; eligibility is established at the point of service by the screening instrument, so reported service figures are counts, not estimates.
